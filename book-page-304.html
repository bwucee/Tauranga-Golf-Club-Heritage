• The Duckmanton GTC Trophy awarded to the player returning the best Stableford score on Past/Present Committee Day. Ivy Duckmanton was a former President and Captain of the Ladies Club. • The Elva Godfrey Trophy awarded to the player returning the best Stableford score in the Christmas Hamper Tournament. Elva also served as Ladies’ Captain and President. • The Memory Vase awarded to the player aged 65 or over returning the best Medal round during the season. Jean White presented this trophy in memory of her mother Margaret Mowat.
