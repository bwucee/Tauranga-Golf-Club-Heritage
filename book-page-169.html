Greg completed his schooling at St. Kentigen’s College in Auckland before signing on with a Scottish professional, Graham Gordon at the old Remuera Golf Club. On completing the apprenticeship he played the Australian Professional circuit for four years with no more than moderate success. The great Australian player and noted teacher Norman von Nida coached Greg for a period at that time.

Having never quite achieved the promise the selection committee saw in him Greg returned to Australia in 1980 to open a Sporting Goods Store, later returning across the Tasman again to take up a position with the Mount Maunganui Golf Club. He now holds the position of Professional with the Carrington Golf Club in Northland.

During his time at Tauranga Kevin Wiltshire, one of three brothers who graced our junior ranks served an apprenticeship with Greg before going on to a successful career as Professional to the Ngaruawahia Golf Club.

ALLAN SNAPE

A P (Allan) Snape was appointed to the position in 1980. Allan was very promising at a range of sports during his school days in New South Wales during which time he played at representative level in rugby, cricket, tennis, athletics, and golf. This was at a time when boys were less inclined to specialise in one sport at the expense of all else. Many would argue that the former practise tended to produce more rounded individuals than is often the case today. On leaving school Allan attended Business College where he gained an excellent grounding in salesmanship and general management skills. These were to serve him well as these are the areas that often let down those who serve as Club Professionals.

For nine years the Australian Golf Company PGF employed Allan. In those years he gained invaluable insight into the finer points of Golf equipment.

A two-year working holiday to the United Kingdom and Europe followed in 1966. In the UK Allan won a prestigious Amateur tournament called the Mosely Golf Open before returning to New South Wales where he gained representative honours and won a number of tournaments including an Australian Foursomes title.
