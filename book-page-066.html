K D (Keith) Dawkins was an excellent cricketer who rapidly adapted to golf despite a grip that the experts frowned upon. Natural talent can overcome a lot. Keith was in his prime years as a golfer when he won the Senior Championship on four occasions during the five years from 1963 to 1967. Only Doug Furrie intervened to prevent Keith sweeping five in a row. In 1972 and 73 Keith had a taste of committee work as Vice Captain to Keith Frith. This was a most useful apprenticeship in Match Committee work. In 1983 Keith again got the call and completed another year as Vice Captain to Bob Allen before taking on the Captaincy in 1984.

Again in 1995 when it became difficult to find anyone to stand as Captain Keith volunteered to fill the position. As would be expected he led the Match Committee through another successful season. In his half-century and more of membership Keith served the Club with distinction in his own quiet manner. A member of the Eagles Golfing Society Keith was elected to Life Membership of the Tauranga Golf Club in 2000.

E A (Ernie) Craig was elected to Life Membership in 2007. The vital role played by Ernie in ensuring that the administration of the Racecourse Reserve continued to serve the interests of the two primary tenants has been detailed in Chapter 3. While it is difficult to visualise what may have happened had Ernie not been so diligent in pursuing his plans for the future administration of the Reserve we can be confident that the Club would have found themselves in a much less secure position than it now holds.

The Racing Club also honoured Ernie with Life Membership in recognition of his work in the interests of the Reserve. D G S (David) Mowat has been a Club member for well over 50 years. He was first elected to the committee in 1967 and served as Captain in 1969 and 70, as Vice President in 1971, and as President in 1972 and 73. In 1989 he was again elected to committee, serving until the Board became a reality in 1994. Four years later he was encouraged to stand for one of the elected positions on the Board. In 1999 David was appointed to the Board Chairmanship, a position he held for 3 years.

In recognition of his contribution to Club administration over an extended period David was honoured with Life Membership in 2006. In over 100 years 23 men have been made Life Members of the Club. While all of these men have richly deserved the honour bestowed upon them by a grateful Club there are many others who have contributed much over the years. The names of many of these will be found scattered throughout this history. Below we pay homage to a selection of those whose service added real value to their Club over the years and we apologise to those many others who must, for one reason or another, go unrecorded, though not unappreciated.

C W (Colin) Johnstone joined the committee as Vice Captain in 1956, graduating to Captain in 1958. Elected Vice President in 1960 Colin assumed the Presidency in 1963.
