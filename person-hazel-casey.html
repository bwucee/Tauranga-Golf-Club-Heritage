PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Hazel Casey

At eleven, Hazel Casey learned golf on a farm course near Ōpōtiki where cows kept the fairways short and her first subscription was five fruit tins to line the holes. At Tauranga she helped beginners earn their handicaps, organised raffles to pay for lessons and took charge of the Saturday women’s nine-hole group.
