PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Alan Davidson

Alan Davidson helped put the Harris course plan into practice during his second term as club president. On tournament days he took on work that players rarely saw. For the Tauranga Open, he and fellow volunteers gave up their chance to play so they could check cards and post scores by hand in time for prizegiving.
