Trundler Shed hospitality centre being well received by the majority of members. The atmosphere created in that centre owed much to the tolerance and cheerful cooperation of our caterers Peter and Tania Robb, our Bar Persons, and to our Professional Rick Barker who was required to run his business in reduced premises and was inconvenienced in a number of other ways.

When the new facility opened in April 2001 with a champagne breakfast the membership at large, including some who had been sceptical earlier, were delighted with the appearance and overall ambience of their new clubhouse. General Manager Grant Scoones who had been instrumental in developing the original concept and in driving its progress, the entire Facilities Advisory Group who had planned and monitored every step of the development, and the Board members, none of whom ever wavered in their belief in the value and viability of the new look clubhouse, could all be justly proud of the part they had played in bringing an ambitious concept to fruition.
