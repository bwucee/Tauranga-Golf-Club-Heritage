OUTSTANDING TAURANGA CLUB PLAYERS

M N (MICHAEL) NICHOLSON Michael began playing golf following a Football (Soccer) accident when he was 14. A promising footballer Michael took up golf in an attempt to put power back into his damaged wrist. Football’s loss proved to be Golf’s gain with Michael reducing his handicap to 1 within two years and never returning to his promising football career. Michael was unusual in his approach to practice as he spent untold hours on the tiny practice fairway between the 11th and 18th fairways hitting wedge shots. After some time his wedge had virtually no grooves left on its face. Somehow the timing and power he learned to put into playing these wedge shots carried over to the longer clubs, even to the driver.

Throughout his competitive career Michael’s ability to play recovery shots was legend. Where he learned to play these is a bit of a mystery but learn them he clearly did. A great strength of his game was the ability to visualise the way the ball would behave in unusual situations. Michael stands supreme amongst those who have represented both the Tauranga Club and the Bay of Plenty. At Club level he won our Senior Championship on 12 occasions and headed our Pennant team for at least 25 years. While based in Whakatane he won their Senior Match play Championship in 1978, 79, and 80. He added the Whakatane Stroke Play title in 1977, 78, and 79.

To further demonstrate his command of the Whakatane course Michael won the Bay of Plenty Open there in 1970, 74, 77, and 82. At Bay of Plenty level Michael set a record of 20 consecutive appearances in the National Inter-Provincial Championship variously known as the Freyberg Rose Bowl, The Government Life Tournament, and later The Tower Tournament when Government Life was renamed Tower Insurance. Michael’s first appearance in the team was in 1970 when he was just 16 years old, his last in 1989. In all he played 136 matches winning 64. In the great majority of these matches he was playing at number one and therefore meeting the best golfers each rival team put forward.

In all Bay of Plenty representative fixtures Michael played 260 individual matches. At National Championship level Michael’s record reads: 1971 NZ Under 18 Champion. 1972 NZ Boys Champion. Under 23 Men’s NZ Champion. 1973 NZ Amateur Champion - also Top Qualifier. NZ Foursomes. 1974 North Island Amateur Champion. 1977 North Island Amateur Champion. NZ Foursomes. 1984 NZ Mixed Foursomes. At International level Michael was selected for the New Zealand Junior Team in 1971 and 73. In 1974 he represented NZ at the Eisenhower Tournament held in the Dominican Republic, and in 1975 he was a member of the NZ team that contested the R and A Trophy at Royal Durban Golf Club in South Africa.

Further National honours came in 1975 with selection in the Sloan Morpeth team to play Australia. Michael ended his long association with the Tauranga Golf Club when he joined the Professional ranks, not as a player, but as a Club Professional with the Mount Maunganui Club. After some years at Mount Maunganui Michael moved to Australia late in 2009 where he joined the Brosnan Group as Manager of their Golf World Branch in Kawana in Queensland. All those who were associated with Michael during his time in Tauranga will long remember him with respect and admiration.
