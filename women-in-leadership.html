1974–2000

Women at the committee table

From Nen Sim’s election to a woman president, representation changed the club’s leadership.

Evelyn, known as Nen, Sim became the first woman elected to the club executive in 1974. The book describes her practical contribution and her concern for the interests of the whole membership.

A 1978 decision provided for a woman appointed from the women’s section as well as those elected directly. Loeta Hutcheson and Peg Cross were among those who followed that route.

When the Board structure arrived in 1994, women again took part in its development. Dave’s account records Joyce Paris among the first elected Board members and Noeline Shiel as vice president.

Peg Copestake became club president in 2000, the first woman to hold that office. The original administration chapter follows the transition in greater detail, including the discussions about responsibilities and representation.
