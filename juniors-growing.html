1960s–2014

Making room for the next generation

Talent and encouragement gradually challenged the restrictions once placed on junior members.

The book includes memories of juniors being challenged over their right to compete or to use parts of the clubhouse. A later recollection describes Wayne Holden and Brent Sutherland playing extra holes in a senior championship match during the 1960s.

Another story recalls Michael Nicholson sitting with adult playing partners at a table placed across the boundary between the clubhouse’s carpet and linoleum, so he could comply with the rule excluding juniors from the lounge.

Against those restrictions stand the people who encouraged younger players, including Una Wickham, James Mowat and family members. The later profiles trace players such as Jay Carter through junior golf and into wider roles.

These memories are retained as accounts of the club’s changing culture. They offer a foundation for a Juniors collection that can also record today’s players, teams and opportunities in their own time.
