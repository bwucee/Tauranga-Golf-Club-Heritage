1990 One can imagine the delight experienced by Brenda Ormsby when her long quest for success in this tournament was rewarded with a 3/2 win over Otago’s Jan Scandrett in the final. Brenda played in the first Tauranga Match Play Open in 1976 and in most of those held since. Runner up on four occasions Brenda had missed the previous two tournaments. This year she may have been in more relaxed mood as she defeated Gina Scott, Sheree Higgens (now married to successful New Zealand professional David Smail), and Ingrid van Steenbergen on her way to the final.

The promising young Tauranga player Anne Rodgers, sister to Catherine, did very well to defeat three very well performed players, including Lynnette Brooky (Grange), in winning the Championship Plate after bowing to van Steenbergen in the first round. Earlier in the tournament Brooky had aced the fifth hole and almost repeated the feat at the fourth in her match with Rodgers, the ball finishing just centimetres from the hole after almost completing a ‘slam dunk’.

1991 The tournament this year was recognised as an official New Zealand trial. This guaranteed an exceptional field including the entire National squad of nine players. Ultimately the two top ranked entrants met in the final after relatively straight forward wins in the preliminary matches. Left hander Lisa Aldridge (Paraparaumu), playing in her first Una Wickham Tournament defeated four time finalist and previous winner Ingrid van Steenbergen at the 19th hole by sinking a 2m. birdie putt. Ironically this putt was almost identical to the putt holed by van Steenbergen at the first hole of the match for birdie. It was Ingrid’s third visit to the 19th hole in her four finals.

Of these she had won just that once in 1988.

1992 Brenda Ormsby had a lot less work to do in winning her second Una Wickham Match Play Title than had been the case when winning her first two years previously. In grand form throughout the tournament Brenda needed only 57 holes to dispose of her four opponents, only Sandra Carey of Putaruru getting close when going down 2/1. Anne Familton (Grange) went down 5/3 in a final where Brenda’s consistency left few opportunities for her opponent to win a hole.

1993 Two of New Zealand’s most talented young players, Gina Scott (Kawhia) and Shelley Duncan (Otago) reached the final following preliminary play that featured many close contests. These two were the only New Zealand squad members competing this year and fittingly came through to meet in the final where Scott prevailed two up in a very entertaining final. Gina Scott in particular thrilled the gallery

with the length of her drives. These were consistently around the 230 - 240m mark. As an example of her power she was able to reach the 17th green with a seven iron second following her drive from the blue tees. When just 20 years old Scott had won the Waikato Champion of Champions title in 1991. Later she was to play in the Professional ranks in Europe.

This 1993 Una Wickham Tournament introduced an eleven year old from Whitianga to the Tauranga public. Julia Kraschinski started golf as a nine year old and qualified for this tournament when she reduced to a twelve handicap. Playing in the fourth sixteen she defeated four much older players in winning the group. It must have been quite unnerving to be outplayed by one so small and so young.
