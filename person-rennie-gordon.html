PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Rennie Gordon

Playing as Rennie Daines, she won three consecutive women’s club championships from 1920 to 1922. She later served as captain and president of the women’s club. By 1961, as Rennie Gordon, she was helping gather material for an early club history, some of which found its way into Mowat’s book.
