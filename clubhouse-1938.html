1938

A clubhouse of their own

The shared tin shed gave way to a dedicated clubhouse, a milestone recorded in photographs.

The first Gate Pa clubhouse was a corrugated iron building shared with racing. The history describes crowded seating, shared lunches and the small comforts members gradually added to it.

In 1938 a dedicated clubhouse was built on the area the 2014 book identifies as the car park. Photographs record its opening and show Miss Eva Goodyear beside the trophies.

Two further views show the wooden building and the open ground around it. The building date is known, but those views are not individually dated. They show the clubhouse built in 1938 without establishing that each photograph was taken that year.

After the war the building could no longer comfortably serve the enlarged membership. When a new clubhouse opened in 1954, the old wooden structure was sold and moved to Pyes Pa, where the book says it became a home again.
