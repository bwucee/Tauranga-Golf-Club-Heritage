Honours, office holders and trophies

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

• The Tauranga Stableford Trophy awarded to the player returning the best aggregate from three of four Stableford rounds. • The Sandlant Candelabra awarded to the most improved player. • The Hazel Casey Pitch ‘N Putt Cup. • The Alison Holdem Trophy awarded to the handicap Match Play winner. • The MacMurdo Cup awarded to the winners of the Four Ball Match Play competition. A keen Nine Hole member, Suzanne MacMurdo, donated this.

Other trophies contested through the season include: • The Crossman Trophy awarded to the player scoring the best aggregate over any six stableford rounds during the season. Donated by former committee member Jo Crossman. • The Silson Trophy awarded to the top qualifier in the Elsie Hanna Cup. Nell Silson gave this silver jug to commemorate the staging of the Russell Grace Inter-Provincial Championship in Tauranga in 1973. Nell was a previous member of the Ladies’committee. • The ‘Score of the Year’ Trophy awarded to the player returning the best 18 hole gross score over the season. This unique trophy features the medal awarded to Miss Una Wickham when representing New Zealand at the Commonwealth Tournament. • The Tui Cup donated by Tui Harvey for Summer Handicap Match Play. • The Marillier Trophy for the best aggregate over six Stableford rounds during the summer season.

Donated by Lynn Goodhue (nee Marillier). Lynn served as both Captain and President of the Ladies Club. • The Hazel Casey Trophy awarded to the winners of the Nancy McCormick Foursomes. The 36+ women pooled their resources to provide this trophy in appreciation of the support given to them by Life Member Hazel Casey. Nancy McCormick of Auckland was appointed secretary to the LGU in 1930 and held that position until 1952. She was one of the most influential figures in the history of women’s golf in New Zealand. The Foursomes competition that commemorates her name is contested throughout the country. • The Jean Reid Trophy is awarded to the player adjudged to be the most improved over the season.

Jean and her husband Jim were active and popular members over many years. Jean served on the Women’s committee and as Women’s Captain in the Sixties. She also filled the position of caterer for several years. Jean also

presented the Tui Cup (not to be confused with the Tui Summer Cup). The former is awarded to the Saturday Woman scoring the best aggregate over two rounds of Stableford competition. • The Cronulla Cup is awarded to the player with the best Stableford score on Captain’s Day. Members of the Cronulla Golf Club of Sydney, in appreciation of the hospitality shown by our Club when they visited, presented this trophy. Cronulla was the Club where Greg Holst had been employed prior to his appointment to Tauranga.
