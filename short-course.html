2014

A new place to learn and practise

The short-course facility opened another way into the game at the end of the period covered by the book.

The final chapter begins with a concrete development: a short-course and practice facility on land beside the second fairway. The book credits legal and planning work that made use of the area possible.

Club champion Dave Cooke and professional Jay Carter designed a flexible layout for pitch-and-putt play, teaching and practice. Synthetic tees and greens were combined with Windsor Green fairways prepared by club staff.

The account acknowledges a Lion Foundation grant arranged through Michelle Towersey and Chris McAlpine. It describes the facility as an opportunity whose future use would depend on the people promoting and using it.

After this section, Dave turns to an imagined account of the next century. The website separates that imaginative ending from the historical timeline: predicted events in 2020 or later are not presented as facts.
