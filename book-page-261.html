THE 2007 CARRUS TAURANGA OPEN CHAMPIONSHIP

With Josh Geary not able to return to Tauranga to defend his title it was left to Auckland Amateur Leighton James and Taihape Professional Hamish Robertson to emulate his opening round 63 of the previous year. Amateurs Josh Munn (a 16 yr old from the Manawatu), Sean Roach (Arrowtown), and Mike Wilson (ex BOP team member) all shot 65s, a total matched by Professional Brad Shilton.

James and Robertson continued to set the pace when each shot 67 in the second round while Mark Purser, a Waikato Professional, came into contention with a magnificent 61. This included two eagles, six birdies, and a single bogey. This year the 17th was correctly rated as a par four so par was set at a more realistic 70. This round elevated Purser to equal third with local man Jared Pender, later to

be selected to represent New Zealand at the Eisenhower Tournament. Jared’s scores of 67 and 65 represented his best form at this level.

A second 63 from Leighton James appeared to have put the Championship out of reach of the others with the final round to come. While a pair of Amateurs again led the field the question of who would take the money was yet to be decided. Purser was not prepared to allow James the title without a fight and, in a dogged pursuit he finally got his nose in front to win by a single stroke. To achieve this he had

to shoot a seven under par 63 while James slipped to a one over 71. Kevin Smith, such a prominent contender the year before, headed all but one of the Professionals to finish third. An inexplicable four- putt at the 15th ended his challenge though his third round 61 ranked with Purser’s earlier 61 as the rounds of the tournament. Robertson was 12 strokes worse than that in his third round to lose any chance he had of heading the field. Purser and James were level with two holes to play but Purser stole the initiative at the 17th when he hit a wedge from 128m and canned the resultant 3m putt.

James could only par the final two holes to finish a valiant second.

THE 2008 CARRUS TAURANGA OPEN CHAMPIONSHIP

Sublime conditions, immaculate fairways, and fast greens presented a quality field with a matchless opportunity to create records on the opening day of the championship. While many threatened to do so none succeeded. Kevin Chun, seven under with four to play, Michael Hendry, his score spoilt by one double bogey, and Amateur Scott Johnson all shot 65s to set the pace. A trio of Amateurs including 2007 runner-up Leighton James came in at five under. 13-year-old Cecilia Cho led the women on even par 71. Hendry held his form through the second round to establish a three-stroke advantage over the field.
