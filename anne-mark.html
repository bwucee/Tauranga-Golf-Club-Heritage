1935–1955

Anne Mark: playing and making things happen

A player and administrator whose contribution reached well beyond her own competitions.

Anne Mark joined the club in 1935. Her recollections include helping Elsie Hanna prepare catering by gathering kindling and cutting firewood for the racing club’s range.

She first served as handicap manager in 1938 and held several positions on the Ladies’ committee. Her account in the book describes a practical involvement in the daily work that kept the club going.

Anne also assisted the Veterans’ Society with its first five-day tournament in 1953, working alongside Rennie Gordon, later Wallis. The Veterans’ history credits the two women with the clerical organisation behind those early tournaments.

In her account, Anne places the formation of the Bay of Plenty Ladies’ Golf Association in 1954 and records serving as its president for three years. She joined the national Ladies’ Golf Union council in 1955. Those roles connect the local club’s story to the wider administration of women’s golf.
