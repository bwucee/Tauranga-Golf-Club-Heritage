1998 Twin sisters Angela and Wendy Hawkes of Titirangi met in the second round, one of many times these two were to meet in important tournaments. An excellent match went to the 20th before Angela prevailed. Angela then defeated International René Fowler 3/2 in the semi-final and defending champion Tina Howard 2/1 in the final. Angela’s victory was well received by the gallery as Wendy and Angela had been loyal and popular supporters of the tournament

1999 Responding to changes in the National Diary of Events the organising committee opted for a change from the traditional February dates to a weekend in August. Whether or not this was a wise decision is open to debate but it does appear to have heralded a decline in the quality of the field and the prestige of the tournament. While this year the tournament attracted most members of the Bay of Plenty team selected for the Inter Provincial Championships there were few quality players from elsewhere. In a rather ordinary final, which perhaps was indicative of the quality of the field, Sandra Baxendine (Matamata) defeated Sue Bassett (Te Puke) one up.

Local interest centred on the performance of Chelsea Cox, daughter of Vanessa Barker, wife of Club Professional Rick Barker. Chelsea must have caused some concern to the Bay selectors as she defeated two of their team before losing a close semi-final match with Baxendine one down.

2000 The tournament date was changed to June. That suited Tina Howard, now a member of the New Zealand National Team and on a Golf Scholarship to the Louisiana State University. Home for a break Tina won her second Una Wickham title by defeating Robyn Pellow (Ngaruawahia) 2/1 in the final. This meant Tina had made the final in all four of her appearances in the tournament, winning two of those.

2001 That year the tournament regained status as an official trial for the Waikato and Bay of Plenty teams. Brenda Ormsby on plus 3 and Tina Howard on plus 1 were the highest ranked players while thirteen of the Championship flight contenders were on handicaps of 3 or better. Jan Storey of Waiterimu was in giant killing form, disposing of Penny Newbrook (Springfield), Wendy Hawkes (Arrowtown) at the 21st, and Brenda Ormsby (Te Puke) at the 19th. Tina Howard had a much easier passage to the final winning her matches comfortably. Storey continued her form into the final to reach the end of the regulation 18 holes all-square. Down the 19th in gathering gloom went the pair only to halve that hole.

Recognising that the gloom would make the 2nd hole virtually unplayable the referee obtained the consent of the committee to play the 18th as the 20th hole. Though Tina raised an objection play

resumed on the 18th where enterprising Club members lined up their cars to cast some light on the green and approaches. Again the hole was halved and the players, now facing almost total darkness agreed to share the title - a bizarre ending to an entertaining final.

2002 Wendy and Angela Hawkes were probably the best performed women in the field and they met in the semi-final where Wendy prevailed 3/2. It was unfortunate that these two seemed destined to meet before the final in so many tournaments. It would have been interesting to see them meet in a final. Wendy continued on her winning way to score a 2/1 victory over Akane Iijima of Pakuranga in a final that was closely contested throughout.
