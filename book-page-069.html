MEN’S REPRESENTATIVE GOLF

As mentioned earlier Tauranga won the BOP Senior Inter Club Shield in 1951 with the help of two outstanding players in Jack Bateman and Fred Ward. It was not until the Michael Nicholson era beginning in the Seventies that Tauranga again enjoyed success at that level, annexing the Shield in 1974, 75, and 76, before becoming the first winners of the Championship Pennant Series in 1978. During this period a talented team of ‘Young Guns’ supported Michael. The team included John Ward, Jim Lancaster, and Noel Johnson. All these represented the Bay of Plenty with distinction at Inter Provincial level. Through the Eighties Mt. Maunganui reigned virtually unchallenged in the Championship Pennant Series so Tauranga had to wait until 1993 to again taste success.

Further wins followed in 1994, 96, and 99. In addition to the four mentioned above prominent team members involved in those wins included Bruce Burbery, Eddie Burgess, Jay Carter, Blair Murdock, and Gavan MacFarlane. The victorious 1996 team were Runners Up in the Johnnie Walker National Inter Club Championship held in Wellington. That team comprised Mike Cannell, Martin Hall, Andrew McNair, Eddie Burgess, Mike Nicholson, and Blair Murdock. Stuart Browne managed the team throughout the season. This Wellington visit may well have been the last occasion on which a Tauranga team was outfitted in our official Club blazers. These had to be borrowed from a group of older Club members, as few, if any, more recent members now owned them.

Further success at BOP Senior Championship level came in 2006 and 2008 featuring new players including Michael Melling, Andre Arthur, George Kinghorn, Scott Trebilco, Jared Pender, Matt Moorehead, Kevin Smith, and Toby Pudney. Eddie Burgess added his experience to this mix. 2011 proved to be a vintage year for our Senior Pennant players with both the Championship, and the Senior A teams winning their BOP finals. For the Championship team the precocious talents of Sam Davis, David Feeney, Karl Knedler, and Ben Shanly were reinforced by the experience of Eddie Burgess, Blair Murdock, George Kinghorn and John Waretini. Karl’s father Peter ably managed the team throughout the season.

A judicious mix of youth and experience also featured in the Senior A team where Jason Ornberg and John Laing provided the experience, James Cannon, Jin Hwang, Oliver and Will Coxhead, the youth. John Laing, a one-time stalwart of Kaiangaroa golf managed this team together with playing for it.

At Intermediate and Junior Pennant level Tauranga have been much less successful. During the 19 years of Shield competition Tauranga failed to win at Intermediate level. It was not until 1980, 30 years after BOP Inter Club competition began, that a Tauranga Intermediate team was successful. Under the system prevailing prior to the present Zone structure Tauranga met Opotiki in the final at Omanu. This day was so memorable in our history it is worthy of further comment. Captained by Norm Robson, the team included Syd Carswell, Ashley Norman, Peter Chadwick, and David Mowat. The Tauranga team became very concerned when Ashley had not appeared half an hour or so prior to tee off.

Someone got on the phone only to wake up a somewhat hungover Ashley who flew into his clothes before breaking all records in driving from Otumoetai to Omanu. Norm Robson, teeing off one group ahead of Ashley, caused a delay by stopping near his ball on the first fairway and beginning a search for his car keys that he claimed to have lost.
