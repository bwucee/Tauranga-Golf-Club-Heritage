None of this development would have been possible had it not been for a grant from the Lion Foundation arranged by Club Manager Michelle Towersey and BOP Golf Chief Executive Chris McAlpine. This grant covered all direct costs so the only cost to our Club was through the use of our staff to establish Windsor Green turf throughout the area.

The promoters of the Short Course Facility envisage a wide variety of activities taking place on the area over the coming years. Time will tell how much of this comes to pass. Whatever the final usage the facility should prove a valuable adjunct to the main course limited only by the imagination of those who use it and promote it.
