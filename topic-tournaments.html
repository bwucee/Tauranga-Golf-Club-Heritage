From the 1968 programme

1968 · PROGRAMME

The 1968 Spalding Masters

The programme schedules 54 holes on 8–9 January, with a $2,000 purse. Its blank field sheets list entrants; results are supplied separately by the club history.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Visiting players in the 1968 programme

Named photographs include Walter Godfrey, Guy Wolstenholme, Martin Roesink, Billy Dunk, Brian Boys, Frank Malloy and other competitors.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Noel Hockin and the L. C. Smith Rose Bowl

A profile of regional and club service. The January 1968 programme presents Hockin as still serving, whereas the book gives a 1967 retirement.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Professionals at the earlier Rotorua Masters

The named group photograph reproduced in the 1968 programme is from the earlier Rotorua event, not Tauranga’s 1968 field.
