CONTEMPORARY SOURCE · JANUARY 1968

The Spalding Masters programme

A source for tournament history, course care, individual lives and Tauranga’s wider community. Each part is indexed under its relevant subjects.

Reading this source

Open the complete searchable programme, including every page and the advertisements. The page links below open it at the relevant place. Printed page 1 is PDF page 3.

Contemporary statements and later recollections may differ. The original book remains available alongside these additions.

The 1968 Spalding Masters

The programme schedules 54 holes on 8–9 January, with a $2,000 purse. Its blank field sheets list entrants; results are supplied separately by the club history.

Honours, trophies & rules

Early rules reproduced in the programme

Seventeen rules attributed by the programme to St Andrews in 1812. Their stated provenance is a copy displayed by Harry T. Moore; they are not Tauranga’s tournament rules.

Spalding Masters official programme, January 1968 · Programme p. 35.

Tauranga in 1968 through its advertising

Shops, clothing, motels, dining, building trades and transport give the tournament a wider social setting. Inside-cover travel and appliance advertisements and the back cover add further context.
