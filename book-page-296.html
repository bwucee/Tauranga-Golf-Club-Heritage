1955/56 D E Coutts 1971/72 D G S Mowat 1993/95 J G Baker 1957/58 C W Johnstone 1973/79 K Frith 1996/02 No Position 1959/61 R G Bathgate 1980/81 R B Eaton 2003/04 J G Baker 1962 R S Lucas 1982/83 I R W Allen 2005/08 K Steward 1963/64 D M Collins 1984/85 K D Dawkins 2009/13 M J Smith 1965/66 J Reid 1986/87 K Frith In 1986 D Cotteral was elected to the position but failed to take up the office. Members were pleased to accept an offer from Keith Frith to fill the breach for a further term.

Up until 1996 the Captain, together with other executive members, had been elected by the full senior membership (men and women). This person was effectively the Men’s Captain and his chief function was as Chairman of the Men’s Match Committee that was known simply as the Match Committee. There was irony in the situation where the women members could, in theory, decide who should be in charge of men’s competitive golf. In 1996 a new position of Club Captain was constituted distinct from that of Men’s Captain. The new appointee was to be a member of the Board and would chair the Player’s Committee that was introduced to oversee and coordinate all golf played on the course.

As a corollary the men would now elect their own Captain and Match Committee just as the women had done since 1937.
