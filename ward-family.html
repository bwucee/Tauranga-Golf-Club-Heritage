1947–2014

A family woven into club life

The Ward family story spans championship golf, committee work and the next generation.

Fred Ward’s arrival in 1947 began a long family association with Tauranga Golf Club. His championship and open-tournament record made him a central figure in the postwar playing story.

His brothers Allen and Claude, widely known as Buster, later joined the club. The chapter follows their playing careers and the wider contributions of the family.

John Ward joined as a junior in 1967 and went on to provincial representative golf and club titles. Errol’s involvement included service as club captain around the turn of the century.

The original family chapter gives the relationships, results and memories in fuller form. It provides a model for later family contributions: a connected account with named people and sources, rather than isolated names on an honours board.
