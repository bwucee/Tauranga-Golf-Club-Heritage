1976–2014

Remembering Una through competition

The women’s match-play tournament became a memorial to a player who had inspired the club.

The book follows the women’s open match-play tournament from its first staging in 1976. After Una Wickham’s death in 1983, the women’s committee developed the event in her memory.

Its fields drew leading players from across the northern regions and, for a time, provided a useful test for interprovincial selectors. The year-by-year account names finalists, winners and close matches.

Dave also reflects on the later difficulty of sustaining the event’s profile as players’ priorities and programmes changed.

The original tournament chapter preserves the detail behind the memorial. It connects naturally with Una’s profile and the wider women’s collection, while keeping the tournament’s origins distinct from the later memorial name.
