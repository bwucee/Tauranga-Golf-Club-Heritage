The Ward dynasty

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

← Previous pageBook p. 228 / 305Next page →
From the book: original views, recollections and terminology are retained. References to “today”, rules, staff or programmes describe the time of writing. Text extraction is being reviewed; use the page reference when suggesting a correction.

Fred’s departure very nearly coincided with the arrival in Tauranga of his brothers, Allen and Claude who were quick to join the Club. The latter would be much more readily recognised by the name of Buster. Like Fred they learned their golf in the Manawatu and each showed much of the family golfing ability. Allen made his mark early by winning the Palmerston North Club Championship in 1938 at the precocious age of 16. He played to a low of 3, while Buster did even better to reach as low as 1. Allen joined in 1961 and remained active until his premature death in 1979. He became a committee member in 1971 and proved to be an extremely influential and wise chairman of the Greens Committee, a position he held at the time of his death.

Allen also found time to manage the Bay of Plenty Junior team, a reflection of his interest in the progress in the game of his sons and their peers.

Buster joined in 1962 and remained a member until his death in 2005. He was active almost to the end, being widely recognised as the Patriarch of the Ward Clan who made such an impact with the size of their group at Friday Night Twilight Tournaments throughout many a summer.

J W (John) Ward joined as a junior member in 1967. At Intermediate school he and his friend Noel Johnson had been encouraged to get seriously involved in the game through the influence of Una Wickham who taught at the school. Both eventually went on to represent the Bay of Plenty at Freyberg Rose Bowl level. John represented the Bay from 1972 until 1982 including appearances in the Freyberg team in 1975, 76, 77, and again in 1982. John won the Tauranga Club Senior Stroke play Championship in 1980, 83, and 86, and the Labour weekend Open Championship in 1978. His lowest round at Tauranga was a 66, one better than Uncle Fred’s best.

At his peak John reached a handicap of 1. Errol Ward, John’s older brother, first joined in 1976, remaining a member for three years. When he returned to the game in 1993 he became more involved, so much so that he was elected to the committee in 1998 before serving as Club captain in 1999 and 2000. Errol has successfully tendered for a number of renovation building projects at the Club. Younger brother Kevin was a member for just two years but made a lasting name for himself by scoring holes in one on successive days on 17/18 May 1978 when just 17 years old. These were at the 12th and 5th holes respectively.

Vivienne Phelps, sister to Errol, John, and Kevin, has been a member since 1997 while her husband, Owen, has been a member since 1990. Later recruits to the Club include Annette Bainbridge and her husband Dennis, and Owen Teague. Annette and Owen are the children of Dorothy who was a sister to Fred, Allen, and Buster. The dynasty continues. Many of the above, plus other family and friends, made up the “Ward Mafia”, a group of up to 25 who patronised the Friday Twilight tournaments in the Eighties and Nineties when they were in full swing. Their ages ranged from that of Buster in his eighties to third generation juniors in their teens.
