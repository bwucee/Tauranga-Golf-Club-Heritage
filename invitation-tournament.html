1953–1959

Inviting New Zealand to Tauranga

A national invitation tournament helped show the course to players and spectators beyond the district.

The 1952–53 committee wanted to attract visiting golfers and new members. Working with the New Zealand PGA, it planned a 54-hole invitation tournament for June 1953.

The first field included eighteen professionals and forty-two invited amateurs. Frank Buckler led the professionals with 216, four ahead of Alex Murray. Tori Jones of Taumarunui headed the amateur field by one stroke.

The event returned through the decade. The book records a round of 64 by Alex Murray in 1954, another win for Buckler in 1955, and a commanding performance by amateur Stuart Jones in 1956. Local professional Jimmy Paterson won the professional division that year.

The ambition was both to promote the course and to let younger players see leading golfers in action. The illustrated Labour Weekend group, dated approximately 1950, belongs to the wider tournament culture at the club; it is not a photograph of the 1953 invitation event.
