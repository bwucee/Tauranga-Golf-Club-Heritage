JIM LANCASTER

Jim Lancaster lived adjacent to Pemberton Park. As a boy he was employed to cut Margaret Mowat’s lawn. One day he asked her if he could have an old golf club he saw in the garage. Margaret said she thought she could do better than that and gave him a couple of better clubs that she had. With these he started hitting balls around the Park outside his fence, probably against local by-laws. One day I noticed he used the ‘left hand down’ grip. He said he had only seen Bruce Burbery hit a ball and that was what Bruce did. Jim soon adjusted to a conventional style and in 1969 became the youngest winner of the Senior Championship before Michael Nicholson took that record away from him just one year later.

In that same year of 1970 both Jim and Michael were selected in the Bay of Plenty Freyberg Rose Bowl team to compete in Invercargill, pioneers in a trend that saw representative golf teams getting younger and younger in average age.

At Club level Jim won the Senior Championship again in 1978 and 1988. At one stage he turned Professional in order to run a business at the 9-hole course and Practise Range that operated at Te Maunga opposite Bay Park during the seventies and eighties. Having soon regained his amateur status he continues to play quality golf to this day

MARTIN HALL

Martin was another junior who learnt his golf in Tauranga before making his mark further afield when winning the New Zealand Maori Championship in 1998.

GAVAN MacFARLANE Another quality player to come through the junior ranks at Tauranga Gavan had a golden run in the early nineties, winning the Senior Championship in three of the four years from 1992 to 1995. Gavan represented New Zealand at under-18 level versus Australia in 1994. In 1992 he placed third in the World Junior Stroke Play Championship in Aberdeen, Scotland. Partially as a result he was elected BOP Sportsman of the year. Later he joined the Professional ranks, serving an apprenticeship at the Hamilton Golf Club. Gavan’s father Ian was, for some years, the Manager/Selector of our Senior Pennant team.

JOSH GEARY

Josh learned his early golf at Otumoetai before holding joint membership at Tauranga where, as a fifteen year old in 2001, he contested the final of the Senior Championship. On that occasion the experience of Gavin Smillie proved just a little too much for the young man who, even then, showed much of the quality play that would eventually take him to the Professional ranks. After joining the Mount Maunganui Club Josh continued to demonstrate his liking for the Tauranga course when achieving spectacular success in under-23 events and in the Carrus Tournament held on the course.
