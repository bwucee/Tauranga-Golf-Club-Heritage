Women’s golf

Sportswoman who excelled at Golf, winning the Club Senior Championship in 1961,63, 64, and again in 1970. At National level she and Una Wickham combined to win the 1959 National Foursomes, and in 1962 Olga tied with Susan Grigg of Christchurch in the New Zealand Stroke Play Championship for the Mellsop Cup. Olga was a member of the BOPTV Russell Grace teams of 1964, 68, and 70, and in 1964 she won the BOPTV Senior Championship. In 1976 she shared the same title with Miss E Fleming and Mrs. V Fraher. In later years Olga further demonstrated her sporting prowess with considerable success in Veterans’ swimming events.

NAN WEBSTER became a member in 1952 and was active for many years. She particularly remembered early golfing partners in Cookie Grant, Pat Kidd, Dos Green, Vera Park, Freda White, Ruby Strang, and Maisey Hardy. Her own personal highlight was holing an Iron shot for an eagle two at the tenth hole. In earlier days members took their own cut lunches to golf and it was a pleasant innovation when professional catering was introduced. She enjoyed especially the services provided by Mrs. Polley and later, by Mrs. Anderson. Both these ladies made an indelible impression on those who were members in that period.

JOYCE WATERWORTH joined the Club in 1954 and was competitive at senior level over many years. She represented the BOPTV team at Russell Grace Inter-provincial Tournaments in 1957, 58, 59, and 61. She recalled that there were seven members of the team at that time with no set reserve so one member had to be left out each time. When Anne Mark was the Manager of the team she did not enjoy telling anyone they would not be playing the following day. To overcome that problem Anne would get the message across while the team relaxed in the Motel hot pool by pinching the unlucky one on her bare bottom.

Joyce knew because it happened to her once.

MARY PARKINSON (Nee Shanks) and her sister JO DAWKINS began playing golf in 1957 at which time their father Arnold was still an active player. Harry Bradmore, ever one to encourage the young player, gave Mary a book on Golf for Ladies. This must have set her in the right direction for she went on to win both the Junior and Senior Club Championships and, in 1982, the Senior BOP Champion of Champions title. While Mary and Terry resided in Northland she added the Mt. Denby Senior Championship title to her tally. During her competitive career Mary represented both Bay Of Plenty and

Northland at Inter- Provincial level.

JOYCE EDWARDS joined the Club with husband Roy in the mid-fifties and soon became involved in many facets of club life. She was a committee member as early as 1957 and took on the secretaryship one year later. In 1958 Joyce became Vice-Captain and Handicapper, served as Captain from 1961 to 63 and as President from 1964 to 68. In that final year of Presidency her administrative talents were recognised further afield and she was elected Northern Delegate to the BOPTV Association in 1968, 69, and 70. From 1972-77 she was Vice-President of the Association and served as its President in 1978-79.
