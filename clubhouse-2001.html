1999–2001

The clubhouse rebuilt for a new century

Planning, member expertise and a major financial commitment produced the clubhouse that opened in April 2001.

In February 1999 the Board established a Facilities Advisory Group to examine the clubhouse. Its members brought experience in construction, drafting, engineering, management and cost estimation.

The proposal used the existing foundations and basement rather than building elsewhere on the course. A July 2000 meeting considered the options and financing, including funds made available when the Golf Club Trust was wound up.

During the work, club life continued in temporary accommodation. The book acknowledges caterers Peter and Tania Robb, bar staff and professional Rick Barker for keeping services going through the disruption.

The new facility opened with a champagne breakfast in April 2001. Dave credits both the formal planning and the members who gave their expertise to bring the project to completion.
