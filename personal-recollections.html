Recollections across the decades

In the members’ own voices

Arnold Shanks and other members describe the people, rounds and incidents that official records rarely capture.

The personal-experiences chapter begins with Arnold Shanks, who served as steward and bar manager from 1967 to 1983. His recollections connect farming life, Saturday golf and the everyday hospitality of the club.

Other accounts describe learning the game, caddying, competition, family connections and memorable incidents. Their value lies partly in the different vantage points from which members remembered the same place.

These are attributed recollections, rather than a single independently verified chronology. Where the book signals uncertainty or an approximate date, the website retains that context.

The full chapter is available in the book collection, page by page, with its names and original wording. Search the collection for a person or follow the chapter’s story headings to find a particular account.
