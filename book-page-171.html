RICK BARKER

Rick began his golfing life in Northland at the age of ten when he became a junior member of the Helensville District Golf Club. He recalled being the only junior member at that time, an era when clubs were less welcoming of juniors than is now the case. The junior subscription at Helensville of just 10/- was no indication that juniors were unwelcome at that club however.

Rick won his first open senior event when just 14 years of age and, by age 16, was winning 72-hole open events against opposition much older and more experienced than himself. Rick’s first success at this level was a victory in the Kaipara Open where he headed a former NZ representative and National amateur champion in Bruce Stevens. At the under 18 NZ Boys’ Championship he finished in second place. At Provincial level Rick represented Northland in the Garrard Shield and Freyberg Rosebowl teams of 1975 and 76, and the Auckland Garrard Shield team in the following two years. His amateur career peaked in 1975 when he annexed the NZ Stroke Play Championship and was leading amateur in the NZ Open.

Partnered with Phil Reid, Rick won the NZ Foresomes title of 1976 and later in the tournament he was beaten in the NZ Amateur Plate final.

Rick joined the Professional ranks in 1978 and won his first event, the Waikato PGA Championship, the following year. Rick then tried his luck on the Australian circuit with little success though he did manage a win in a small Pro-Am at Royal Melbourne. Following on from his Waikato PGA win in 1979 Rick won over 40 Pro-Am events throughout New Zealand and the Pacific Islands over the following eight years.

Highlights of Rick’s professional career included: • Third place in the NZ PGA Championship, • Second place in the American Samoa Open, • Third place in the Tahiti Open, • Third place in the Pacific Harbour Open, • Third place in the Raratongan Open, • Winner of the 84-hole Fijian Hotel Pro-Am. On another occasion Rick was credited with the lowest round scored in the NZ Open of that year.

By 1986 Rick had decided that he had probably gone as far as he could as a Tournament Professional and he accepted the job as resident professional at the small Otumoetai Club in Tauranga. Two years later the Tauranga position became available when Alan Snape moved to Omanu and Rick was appointed to the job. For some years he maintained the shop at Otumoetai while serving full time at Tauranga. In 2006 the Club upgraded the shop and, in conjunction with that, the Board revised the role of the Professional so that it would include extended participation in the activities of the club and its membership. Rick did not feel he wished to be involved in this extended role and did not apply for a renewal of his contract.

Consequently he completed his service as the Club’s professional in 2006 to go into an unrelated business.
