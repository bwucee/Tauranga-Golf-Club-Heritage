While the A L Davidson Cup is open to schoolboys belonging to any club the Fraser Cup is open only to schoolboys belonging to the Tauranga Club. It does appear that through changes in administration the concept that only schoolboys are eligible for this competition has been overlooked. Consequently in some years it may have been played as a competition open to Junior Boy members rather than restricted to those still at school. In some years the competition was not held as the administrators were apparently not aware of the existence or purpose of the trophy. The donor of this Trophy, Mr Ken Fraser, was born in Scotland. On arrival in New Zealand he farmed at Mangaweka for some years before moving to Auckland where he established a successful business producing and distributing agricultural chemicals.

Later he moved to Tauranga where he resided for many years. Ken took a keen interest in young people and donated the cup to encourage Junior Boys to work hard at the game. The success of many of those whose names feature above is testimony to that encouragement.
