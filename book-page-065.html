M W (Maurice) White was the son in law of Jimmy Mowat, our first Professional. Maurice was first elected to the committee in 1971. He was elected Vice President in 1974 and President in 1977, filling that role for two years. Maurice returned to the committee in 1988 and remained a committee member until the advent of the Board of Management in 1994. For the majority of his time on committee Maurice chaired the Finance Committee, sometimes as its sole member. Maurice was an early advocate of the Board form of management and was active in promoting the concept and in assisting in setting up the constitutional details to enable it to function.

For his contribution to Club administration over a lengthy period Maurice was elected to Life Membership in 1991. Noel Copestake joined the Club in 1960. Few have given as much to the Club in the course of our history as has Noel. First elected to the committee in 1985 Noel remained there in various roles until the advent of the Board in 1994. Noel assumed the Vice Captaincy in 1989, and the Captaincy a year later. Following three years as Club Captain Noel was elected President in 1993, an honour he held for two years. In the last of these he took his place on the Board in its inaugural year, the constitution providing for the President to be an automatic Board member.

Noel’s contribution to the Club through his promotion of Twilight Golf, and his leadership of the ‘Wednesday Firewood Gang’ further evidenced his dedication to the Club.

In 1984 Noel won the Junior Championship. He has had a long association with the selection and management of Pennant teams over the years. In recognition of his work in the interests of Golf Noel was elected to the Bay of Plenty Eagles Golfing Society in 1995, later becoming their Secretary/ Treasurer.

Noel was elected to Life Membership of the Tauranga Golf Club in 1997, soon to be joined in that elite company by his wife Peg. These two appointments represented a remarkable and unique achievement, one that would have been applauded by every member who had been privileged to experience the service these two gave to the Club over so many years.

Bernie Child came to the Club from the Manawatu where he had worked as a contractor using heavy machinery in his work. A very practical man it was behind the scenes where Bernie flourished. He was project organiser for the ‘Vets’ for a number of years and, organiser or not, he was ever to the fore when work required to be done. Few who have not had the experience of working at some of the many projects Bernie and his peers have completed over the years would appreciate just how valuable

Bernie’s contributions have been. The Club is much the richer for his contribution. We enjoyed his little plaque on the drinking fountain behind the first tee: ‘A fountain of youth built by a child’. The fountain was his idea and entirely his own work. It stands as an indication of the commitment Bernie has to his fellow members. He became involved in administration as a member of the Veterans’ Golfing Society. In 1995 he was honoured with the Presidency of that organisation, a job he did with his characteristic dedication. In recognition of his commitment to his Club, and to his selfless contributions of time and energy over many years, Bernie Child was elected to Life Membership in 1997.
