In 1988 Bert Krause was in the Chair and the Society: • Purchased and installed an additional six ball washers. • Provided a new blade attachment for the Toro mower. • Regraded the route to the fifth green and constructed a trundler park at the same hole. • Repaired and sealed the area by the Pro Shop. • Built bark gardens with mowing strips by tenth tee and beside trundler shed. • Assisted the staff to spray paspalum in eradication program.

In 1989 under President George Mason the Society: • Built a new bark garden adjacent to the eleventh green. • Built an observation tower forward of seventh tee. • Upgraded the first tee. This included installing a block wall. • Assisted staff in cleaning around bunkers prior to Finals Day. • Erected new tee marker boards. • Felled and removed dead trees. • Cleared the old garden in front of the Club House. • Repaired and rebuilt block work on the sixth tee.

In 1990 under President Bill Vinnecombe the Society: • Erected a wind screen along the fifteenth hole boundary. • Planted new trees, particularly on fifteenth hole and behind fourteenth green. • Removed trees felled by storm. • Erected new tee marker boards. • Cleaned and painted trundler shed exterior and Club House veranda. • Assisted staff in spraying for eradication of Cape Daisy. • Improved drainage around 5th and 7th greens and 8th fairway by installing soak holes. • Constructed a pathway to the side and rear of the 7th green.

In 1991 under President Merv Kelly the Society: • Completed ground works around 7th green and 8th tee area. • Rebuilt and repainted the fence behind 8th green and 9th tee. • Supplied and erected pipe rail to ladies 3rd tee. • Maintained gardens, an ongoing project. • Erected new club sign at Cameron Road entrance. • Maintained ball washers, another ongoing project. • Purchased new blue tee markers. • Supplied material and constructed steps to the 2nd tee. • Installed pipe and chain fences on path to Club House.
