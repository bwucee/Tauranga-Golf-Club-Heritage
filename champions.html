1911–1923

Names on the early honours boards

The championship lists preserve a dated thread through years for which other records are incomplete.

A. Matheson won the men’s senior championship in 1911 and 1912, and again in 1921. The introduction explains how the honours boards beginning in 1911 helped that year become accepted for a time as the club’s foundation date. The later research presented in the book instead supports 1904.

The men’s list records H. Phillips in 1913, no championship played in 1914, J. Fowles in 1915 and 1916, R. Renshaw in 1917, H. J. Rowe in 1918, and R. H. Roache in 1919. It gives Renshaw again in 1920, 1922 and 1923. The list does not explain why the 1914 championship was not played.

The women’s senior championship list begins in 1919 with Mrs G. Henderson. Miss R. Daines won in 1920, 1921 and 1922; Miss N. Wallis is listed for 1923. A separate biographical passage identifies the three-time winner as Rennie Daines.

The women’s list starting in 1919 should not be read as evidence that women only began playing then: the book names women members and mixed competition much earlier.

These names close the preview in 1923. The collection can grow into later decades using the same source references and article structure.

Source note

Adapted from Pars from the Past, edited by David G. S. Mowat (2014), pp. 4, 99, 286, 291. Newspaper reports are described as cited in the book; original issues have not yet been checked for this preview.
