MEMBER REVIEW EDITION

Help put more skin on the bones.

Tell us what works, what needs correcting, and which parts of the club’s story we should develop next.

What would be useful?

  • Names, dates or places to correct, with a source if you have one.
  • People missing from a story or a photograph.
  • Ideas for the Juniors, Ladies, Ladies 9 Hole, Men and Senior Men collections.
  • Recent records, photographs or results to preserve.
  • Anything difficult to find or read on your phone or computer.

Leave a note on GitHub

Add feedback ↗

A free GitHub account is needed to submit a note. You can browse the existing feedback without signing in. Feedback there is public.

Prefer to send your thoughts directly?

Reply to the club member who shared this preview with you. Include the page title or link, your suggested change and any source or photograph you would like considered. This website does not collect messages through a form.

Contributing material

Please include the event date, names of people, the club group it relates to, and who supplied the material. For photographs, identify the photographer or owner where known and say whether they have agreed to online use. New submissions will be reviewed before joining the collection.

What this preview covers

Six historical chapters cover the story through 2014. All 25 chapters of Dave’s book, front matter and appendices are available in the book collection, alongside shorter story introductions. The imagined future in the final chapter is labelled and excluded from the historical timeline. New contributors will be credited as the collection grows.

Help this collection grow.

A correction, a name for a face, or a story to add? Your knowledge can make the record richer.
