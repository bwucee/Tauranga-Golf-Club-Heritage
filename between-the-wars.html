1924–1933

A club finding its stride

Photographs and championship records show a growing club before the major changes of the 1930s.

The championship lists offer one way into the interwar years. The men’s senior title went to G. Pott in 1924 and 1926, R. P. Baigent in 1925 and A. Matheson in 1927. The women’s list records Mrs D. V. Hanna in 1924 and 1926, Mrs R. Burke in 1925 and Miss C. Humphreys in 1927.

The book’s profile of Elsie Hanna says she arrived in Tauranga in 1922 and served on the Ladies’ committee from 1926 to 1939. Such service is another part of the record alongside the championship results.

A photograph of closing day in 1931 gathers members outside the jockey’s room, which served as the early clubhouse. Other photographs identify Connie Humphreys in 1927 and a group at lunch in 1932. They give faces and everyday settings to an otherwise sparse written record.

The course still included holes within the racecourse oval. An old scorecard reproduced on page 29 records that earlier layout; the book describes a substantial redesign in 1934. These images and records provide a bridge from the first Gate Pa years to the next phase of development.
