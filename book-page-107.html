Women’s golf

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

The inaugural BOPTV Honda Nine Hole Tournament was held at Tauranga in 1999, thus recognising the lead that Tauranga had assumed in promoting this form of the game.

Women’s Nine Hole Golf is now an integral and important sector of our Club. A full program of organised competition on Tuesday mornings caters to a full field of members in the category. The game is here to stay. The popularity of this form of the game is obvious and it appears there will be no diminution of interest from beginners and older members alike”.

(While nine-hole membership is also available to men there has not been the same response from them. This may be that men are better able to find the time to devote most of a day to their game, or perhaps a more likely explanation is that there has been much less encouragement provided to them. The dedication to the cause of Women’s Nine Hole Golf shown by Phyll Sandlant and her team, and by those such as Alison Holdem and Lynn Goodhew who followed, has clearly been the vital ingredient in the success of the program. In is also clear that the establishment of Nine Hole Golf represented a significant financial bonus to the Club as it provided additional income at a time when clubs throughout New Zealand were finding it difficult to balance their books.)

In recognition of her outstanding contribution to Women’s Golf as Captain, President, and pioneer promoter of Nine Hole Golf, Phyll was elected a Life Member of the Club in 2006.
