Twentyfour players bettered par while 19-year old Jim Lancaster of the host club returned an excellent 70 in his first tournament at this level.

Teeing off in the second round Charles led by just one stroke from Guy Wolstenholme of England and Ted Ball of NSW. He soon showed he meant business with a superb round of 62 featuring eight birdies and no dropped shots, par having been dropped to 70 with the 17th hole now correctly identified as a par four. Charles commented after the round that he “holed every putt within ten feet except one on the 14th, but my only really good birdie putt was on the seventh”. Ted Ball, of the lightning fast swing trailed by seven strokes and was heard to comment: “There is only one man who can stop Charles on this course.

That’s Kel Nagle and he’s in Australia”. How prophetic those words were to prove as Charles compiled rounds of 69 and 63 in the final rounds to finish on 260, twenty under par and ten strokes clear of John Lister in second place.

Meanwhile Marilynn Smith demonstrated that women playing off the back tees on a course set to test the best men would always have difficulty competing against them. Never the less the quality of her ball striking and general play impressed the spectators, both male and female, who were privileged to see her in action. Her best round was a 73.

Paul Hart, an unheralded NSW professional, matched Charles’ record 62 during his third round, a feat witnessed by a mere handful of spectators until word of his deeds spread late in the day. With his other three rounds including a pair of 69s and a 71 Hart tied for third with Bob Shaw and Brian Boys. Stuart Jones made no contest of the Amateur division with a seven under aggregate including a fine 65 in the second round.

When asked by the BOP Times reporter for comment following the tournament the following were representative of the replies received: Bob Charles: “The finest greens in New Zealand”. Dennis Sullivan: “That seventeenth green would grace any course in the World”. Guy Wolstenholme: “They have really worked hard on the course and the greens have been prepared by someone knowledgeable and are as good as anything in New Zealand”. Bob Shaw - recently returned from playing in Europe and the USA. “The only bad green is the 15th. The rest would compare favourably with anything I have played on”. Jess Vaughn (USA): “The course is as good as any I played on in England, the United States, or Australia, and would be on a par with many competition courses in the United States”.

Marilynn Smith: “I found the course very much the same as Overseas, the greens being very true with no hidden tricks”.
