R B (Bob) Eaton joined the Club in 1963. First elected to the committee in 1977 Bob took on the Vice Captaincy in 1978 before filling the Captaincy for three years. Vice President to Keith Frith, Bob moved into the President’s chair in 1983 when Keith moved aside with a sigh of relief. Later, when the Board of Management was in operation, Bob’s experience was again called upon and he was elected to serve a term there. It is probable that the high point of Bob’s golfing career came in 1987 when he was a member of the Junior Pennant team that won the Bay of Plenty final at Lake View.

D W (Dan) Dougherty was elected to the committee in 1989. Following a rather more low key coup than that of 1966 Dan made a rather meteoric rise to become President just a year later, serving in that capacity for three years. During his years as President the Club dramatically improved the quality and range of equipment available to the Greens Staff. For this Dan deserves a lot of credit. The purchase of up to the minute equipment to mow greens, fairways, and surrounds, immediately improved the efficiency of the staff and the appearance and quality of the course. It was a bold step as it meant the Club was forced to borrow heavily to finance purchases but in retrospect it was the right move.

While present and future members continue to carry a burden of debt they also benefit from the better course conditions now prevailing. In the new Millennium Dan became heavily involved in the administrative side of Bay of Plenty Golf as a Selector, Team Manager, and eventually President. He was active during the period when Bay of Plenty became the predominant competitive Province in New Zealand Men’s Golf. Many believe Dan was instrumental in inculcating a spirit of commitment amongst team members that overcame a previous over-emphasis on the social aspects of Tournament golf. This lead to continuing success for Bay teams at National level. Fittingly Dan was made a Life Member of Bay of Plenty Golf, the organisation that arose out of the amalgamation of Men and Women’s golf in the Bay.

Gary Baker joined us from the Manawatu Golf Club. An ideas man, who was prepared to put in the effort to see that many of these came to fruition, Gary was elected to the Vice Captaincy in 1990. He and Noel Copestake made a strong and innovative team heading the Match Committee. When Noel accepted the President’s chair in 1993 Gary took over as Captain, serving two years in that role. With the advent of the Board of Management he took a brief break from committee work until returning as Vice President in 1997. A year later he was elected President, a position he held for the next three years.

As President he was automatically a member of the Board over that period. Gary’s contribution to the initial launch of Business House Golf is detailed elsewhere but it is clear that his influence was pivotal to its success. In the realm of Thursday ‘Ball in the Bucket’ golf Gary was much to the fore through his organisation and his innovative ideas.
