In the following three years she represented the same District in the Masters team before moving to Tauranga in 1995. Anna has been a member of Tauranga Golf Club since 1996 winning the Senior Championship on three occasions with perhaps more yet to come. Anna’s talents were immediately recognised in her new District where she represented the Bay of Plenty at Master’s level for a further twelve years. In her fifteen years of Master’s golf Anna was on the winning side on seven occasions, a remarkable record.

During this period Anna was asked to serve as a selector for New Zealand Women’s Golf becoming convenor of selectors soon afterward. Management of National teams followed involving a number of overseas trips to tournaments such as the Queen Sirikit Tournament, the Espirito Santo World Cup, the Tasman Cup, and a number of Asian tournaments. In 2005 Anna was elected President of the Tauranga Golf Club and continued in that role to become easily its longest serving President. As President she was automatically a member of the Board of Management where her wide experience and her wisdom proven invaluable.

HAZEL CASEY learned to play golf as an eleven year old on a farm course at Waiotahi near Opotiki. Her uncle Jack Carter taught her the game. He was the father of Tauranga members George and Doug Carter, and thus the grandfather and great-grandfather respectively of the men of Golf Fusion, Jack and Jay. The greens at Waiotahi were fenced and the cows kept the fairways to a reasonable height. Hazel was armed with just three clubs, a wood, an iron, and a putter. Her first “subscription’ took the form of five large fruit tins. These were required to line the five holes the course boasted. Hazel had difficulty finding these as few families could afford large tins of fruit in the difficult years of the early thirties.

A couple of years later the family moved the short distance to Nukuhau North where the sub. was five shillings, quite a lot for a schoolgirl to find at the time. After marrying during the war Hazel and Bill later moved to Tauranga where they joined the Mt. Maunganui club in 1963. In 1970 application was made to join Tauranga where membership was restricted and ‘B’ membership only available at the time. Forty one years later Hazel was still an active member. Early on Hazel became involved in assisting Adrienne Coad with helping the 36+ ladies to learn the rudiments of the game and gain their handicaps. Later Ali Smit took over from Adrienne.

No professional lessons were offered the beginners so Ali and Hazel did the best they could to inculcate good swing habits into the group. When Ali left Hazel took over as the 36+ supervisor. During the 2007 Carrus Tournament Emily Perry of Lochiel recorded the best round (65) ever by a woman player on our course. Playing off the blue tees Emily scored birdies at the first; second, fifth, seventh and seventeenth holes, together with an eagle three at the sixth. Her only blemish came at the fourteenth.
