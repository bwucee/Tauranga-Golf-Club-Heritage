PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

David Mowat

Before editing the club’s centenary history, Dave Mowat had already spent decades helping run the club. He was captain in 1969 and 1970, went on to the presidency, and returned to serve as board chair from 1999. For the book he brought earlier research, club records and members’ recollections together in time for the 2014 centenary at Gate Pā.
