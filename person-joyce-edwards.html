PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Joyce Edwards

Joyce Edwards joined with her husband Roy in the mid-1950s and was soon keeping records and handicaps for the women’s club. She went on to captain the women, serve as their president and lead the regional association in 1978–79. Long after those years in office, she was still getting out to play a few holes.
