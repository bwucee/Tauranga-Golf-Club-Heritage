The Tauranga Veterans’ Golfing Society was officially launched in 1952. John McKenzie was the inaugural President with Dick Phelan as his Secretary. To celebrate the Silver Jubilee of the Society in 1977 the then President, G A (Bill) Sanders, and Past President, K C (Ken) McIntyre, produced a history of those first 25 years. I can do no better than to print their history as they presented it to their members at that time and then move on to cover the succeeding 44 years during which the Society has flourished in a manner that would be a revelation and a source of great pleasure and pride to the founding members.

In Bill Sander’s words in his preface to their document: “We hope that all who read this History will be caught up in the spirit of our founders and those who followed them over the past 25 years”. Here then is the history of those first 25 years as compiled by Bill Sanders and Ken McIntyre.

THE BEGINNING

Like most beginnings, what was to become our Veterans’ Society started in a very small and somewhat obscure way. Shortly after World War Two a small group of elderly golfers began meeting by arrangement on Tuesdays and Thursdays to make up friendly Four-balls so as to enjoy some easy going and pleasant golf. Under these idyllic golfing conditions the ranks of those originals began to grow and, with growth, the natural corollary was organisation. Attendance became more regular and someone soon decided that every second Tuesday a draw would be made at 12:45 pm. All who wished to play were asked to report prior to that time. Shades of the past were beginning to cast future shadows!

At this point it is appropriate to ask who were those old timers who started it all. Names start to emerge including Dick Phelan, Gavin Wallace, Tom Long, George Munroe, Harry Bradmore, John McKenzie, Ron Galloway, George Willcock, Len Belk, Jimmy Mowat, Arthur Towsey, and Johnny Walker. Doubtless there were others but if the written record fails how can the best of memories make up for that? Many of those mentioned had served the Golf Club well and four of them, Phelan, Towsey, Bradmore, and Willcock, were made Life Members. We salute those original few who started something that has given such joy and golfing pleasure to so many veterans of the future.
