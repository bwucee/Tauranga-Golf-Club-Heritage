PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Jackie Reynish

Jackie Reynish researched the people and stories behind Tauranga’s trophies, including awards that were no longer presented. She compiled two albums, one for current trophies and one for archived ones, with photographs and details of who presented each trophy. The September 2020 club newsletter pointed members to both albums in the meeting room.
