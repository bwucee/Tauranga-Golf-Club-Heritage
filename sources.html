HOW THIS COLLECTION IS BUILT

The evidence behind the stories.

A working collection, with clear references and room for corrections.

Primary supplied source

Pars from the Past: A History of the Tauranga Golf Club, edited by David G. S. Mowat, published in 2014. Page numbers refer to printed book pages, which match the supplied PDF page numbers.

The source PDF remains outside this website and is not offered for download. The current text is newly written from the source, rather than a reproduction of whole book chapters.

The club’s existing History page

The Tauranga Golf Club History page was consulted on 9 November 2026 as a supporting overview. The book provides the detailed references used here.

Dates and gaps

1903 marks Brown’s arrival, not a claimed club foundation. The book supports 1904 as the foundation year. Its introduction explains the lack of early minute books and the use of recollections and newspaper research. Newspaper originals have not yet been independently checked for this preview.

The undated golfing-party and Avenue images are contextual material. Their inclusion does not establish a 1903–1923 date. The Domain course card also has a dating question noted by the book itself.

Additional source for nine-hole golf

Luke Balvert, “A hole in 100”, SunLive, 15 November 2014, identifies Patsy Hankins’s national role and her approach to Phyll Sandlant in 1998. The nine-hole story distinguishes this report from the book’s local account.

Details still to check

The source notes in the redesign, wartime, clubhouse and Veterans’ stories identify conflicting details within the book. They concern the lease chronology, wartime championship years, final clubhouse cost and a tournament date. These questions remain visible for members to help resolve.

Image register

Each archive record includes its book page, available credit, date status and reproduction authorisation. Images were extracted without generating or replacing historical detail; only web sizing and JPEG compression were applied.

Review edition and image permission

The project owner authorised all photographs and images for publication on 9 November 2026. Check the historical text and identifications with the club and verify the newspaper references where possible as the collection develops. This is a member review edition, shared to invite corrections and development suggestions. Historical editorial approval is not implied by publication of the preview.

Alongside the main website

The existing club History page can remain a short introduction, linking to this collection when ready. This separate static collection does not change bookings, membership, news or the club’s existing navigation. Any future address or hosting arrangement is still to be agreed.
