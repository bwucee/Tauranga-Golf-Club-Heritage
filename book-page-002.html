ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

The production of this book has been greatly facilitated by the advice, encouragement, and financial assistance of many people.

Particular thanks are due to the following:

The Board of Management of TGC who underwrote the final production run of printed copies.

The Committee and Members of the TGC Seniors Golfing Society (Veterans)

who financed the setting up costs of the production.

The Volunteer Firewood Gang for both their financial assistance and their willingness

to offer encouragement.

The staff of Tauranga City Library for their technical assistance in association with the

microfiche copies of the BOP Times they hold.

The BOP Times and the Bay Sun newspapers for their publicity leading up to the Centennial celebrations.

And especially to Sharyn Cooke of Pukeko Print & Design Ltd for her professional expertise, her artistic insight, her patience, and her

personal financial contribution by way of significantly discounting

the setting up costs of producing this book.

Reference has been made to the books “A Centennial History of Tauranga” by W.H. Gifford

and H. Bradney Williams and to “Golf in New Zealand - A Centennial History” by G.M. Kelly.
