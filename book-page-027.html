No. 1 Hole (now 11th) “The Avenue”

B.O.P. Times. Saturday June 20th 1914

GOLF

In Future CARS will leave the corner of Wharf Street and the Strand for the links between 1 and 3 pm on Saturdays. To secure seats players must notify the Secretary on or before Friday night.

W. M. DARLEY

Hon. Secretary
