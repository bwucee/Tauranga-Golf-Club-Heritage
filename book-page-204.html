ARNOLD SHANKS REMINISCES

Arnold joined the club in 1948 and was active as a player until 1967 when he took over the role of club steward and bar manager, a role he filled until his retirement in 1983. Arnold’s courteous manner, his patience, his quiet efficiency, his genuine interest in members and visitors alike, all contributed a great deal to the popularity of the club. His generosity in providing beautiful floral arrangements for the lounge with flowers from his own Bethlehem property was greatly appreciated. All who enjoyed his service and his cheerful friendliness will remember Arnold with affection. Here is his story in his own words.

“I came to Tauranga from Ardglass Northern Ireland in 1927 with my golf clubs - a Mills cleek (like a short driver) and an old mashie. I played initially as a country member and enjoyed Open Tournaments. I farmed at Oropi and had to get feeding-out done a day ahead so I could be free on Saturdays between milkings. The Tauranga course was well laid out and well kept. I did not get any serious practice but played reasonably to an 18 handicap. We enjoyed our golf very much and prizes were not considered very important. At the end of the season we received a voucher covering any successes during the season and we would buy what we wanted and take it out to the Club for prize giving in November.

This was all very convivial though it would be rather impractical today.
