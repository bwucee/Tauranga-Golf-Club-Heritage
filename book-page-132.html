Veteran men’s golf

In 2001 under President Ross Ellison the Society: • Donated $4500 towards purchase of fairway irrigator and the support of Junior Golf. • Installed drinking fountain by 1st tee. The “Fountain of Youth” built by a Child. This project was an independent Bernie Child enterprise.

In 2002 under President Peter Walker the Society:

• Donated $12 000 to the Club. All but $400 of this came from the firewood team.

In 2003 under President John Turnbull the Society: • Carried out regular maintenance work. Mike Champion’s work in maintaining ball washers was especially noteworthy. • Assisted the Club program to replace trees around the course by donating $200 from Society funds. A number of individual veterans contributed independently to that programme.

In 2004 under President Mike Sandlant the Society:

• Provided funds for the construction of an appropriate lectern for use in the clubhouse. Joe Carr voluntarily did the actual woodworking to bring this to fruition. • Improved the surrounds of the toilet block beside the 13th tee.

In 2005 under President Ian Anderson the Society:

• allocated $2000 for the purpose of sealing the path from the 11th tee to the fairway. • painted the rails indicating out of bounds on the 10th hole.

In 2006 under President Bob Diprose the Society:

• assisted in tidying the course prior to the Carrus tournament. • assisted in the coring program by sweeping and removing corings - a regular project.

In 2007 under President Hugh Boughtwood the Society:

• laid a new sealed path from the 5th green to the 6th tee incorporating reconstruction and resealing of all sealed areas around the 6th tee/8th tee complex. This was jointly financed with the firewood team, and supervised by Denis Smith. Peter Bourne was involved in the planning while a number of members provided their labour. • relaid the path alongside the 15th tee by removing invading tree roots and surfacing with

crusher dust.

In 2008 under President Jim Miller the Society: • allocated $2000 for the renovation of the practice nets. This work was carried out in 2009.

In 2009 under President Phill Simpson the Society: • allocated $3000 for an upgrade planned for the 15th tee and surrounds.
