A few ways to begin

These questions are adapted from the supplied Member Nostalgia interview template.

  • When did you join Tauranga Golf Club, and what brought you here?
  • Which golfing personalities or friendships stand out, and why?
  • How has the course changed during your membership?
  • Do you remember a club professional, a lesson or an amusing golfing incident?
  • What were dress standards, clubs and golf balls like when you began?
  • What do you remember of catering, social occasions and club traditions?
  • Which competition or achievement remains especially memorable?
  • What else would you like future members to know?

Share a memory or arrange an interview

Send your recollection to the club member who shared this collection with you, or use the feedback options. Please include your name, when you joined, approximate dates and the names of people mentioned. For photographs, include the photographer or owner where known and permission for online use.

Personal recollections will be credited to the contributor. Historical details will be compared with Mowat’s book and the club’s official history before being incorporated into the wider account.
