JAMES MOWAT

The Rotorua based Professionals, Mr. Hood and Mr. McCormack visited Tauranga in the early years though Tauranga had no resident Professional until 1936 when James Mowat accepted appointment to the position. James was born in Montrose, Scotland in 1895. Montrose lies North of St. Andrews on the coast and is home to one of the oldest golf courses in Scotland. It was founded in 1812 and is reputed to be the ninth oldest course in the World. After playing limited golf as a boy James enlisted in the 10th. Highland Light Infantry in December 1914 and served first in France where he was twice wounded. He was drafted back to Montrose for short convalescent spells at home before returning to the Battlefield.

He ended the war fighting the Turkish forces in Mesopotamia, now known as Iraq. The Battalion was on the way home after the armistice in 1918 when it was diverted to quell an uprising in the Khyber Pass. As a result James was the last of all the men from Montrose to make it home after the war.

The prospect of life as a clerk in the Railways had little appeal to James after his experiences during the war and it was not long before he set sail for the USA where he at first worked for his uncle Bob who owned a shoe business in New York. A year or so later he met up with another man from Montrose and they responded to the growing demand in America for people who had some knowledge of, and experience in, the game of golf. Men from Scotland were highly regarded in that context. Most of Jim’s work in America was involved in Golf Course design though he did fit in some teaching and playing.

His reputation must have been well established as he was on one occasion invited to partner the then US Open Champion Gene Sarazen in an exhibition match in Miami against Leo Diegel (twice winner of the USPGA Championship) and Mike Brady, the latter being widely known as the Bridesmaid on account of his numerous second placings in major championships. In this match Jim scored seven birdies, his side prevailing without the need for Sarazen to contribute anything to improve their combined card.

Major Tournament winners to have played our course at some time in the past include:

Peter Thomson – Five times British Open Champion. Also placed 4th in US Open. Kel Nagle - Centennial British Open Champion. Also 2nd US Open. David Graham - US Open and PGA Champion Top five in Masters and British Open. Tony Jacklin - British and US Open Championships. Billy Casper - Twice winner US Open. Also Masters Champion and 3 times second placed in PGA Championship, 4th in British Open. Sir Bob Charles – British Open Champion. Tied 2nd PGA Championship, Twice tied 3rd in US Opens.
