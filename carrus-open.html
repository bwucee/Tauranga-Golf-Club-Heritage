2006–2011

Professional golf returns

The Carrus Tauranga Open introduced another generation of leading players to the club.

The book describes the club joining a series promoted by Greg Turner, with Paul and Sheryl Adams of Carrus Corporation becoming the name sponsors. The first tournament in this account was held in 2006.

Local amateur Josh Geary won that first event. Dave’s account follows his contest with Kevin Smith and the recovery from a difficult hole in the third round.

Later tournaments brought further memorable performances. Richard Lee’s 59 in 2010 became a landmark round, while the 2011 event ended in a wet final-day battle won by Jim Cusdin.

The original chapter contains the year-by-year narratives, women’s results and local connections. Its coverage ends in 2011; later Carrus results can be added as new records without attributing them to the book.
