PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Peg Copestake

Before taking up golf, Peg Copestake competed as Peggy Smith in hockey, table tennis, indoor basketball and athletics. Within about five years of starting golf, a Bay of Plenty Times clipping records her winning the South Auckland women’s championship and playing off a handicap of one. She later took a trailer of golf equipment to local schools to teach children the game and became the first woman elected president of Tauranga Golf Club.

Life Membership · 2005

From Peggy Smith to a one-handicap golfer

The clipping recalls that Peg represented Auckland in hockey and Bay of Plenty in table tennis, reached the national mixed doubles semi-finals, and placed third in the discus at the New Zealand championships. It also describes how she spent her first three months with golf coach Bronco Branch on lessons and practice before playing on a course. By the time of the article, she had won the South Auckland women’s championship and the J. W. Ellis Challenge Cup in a field of 64.

The clipping has no visible publication date. The article says Peg had started playing about five years earlier and had yet to win a Tauranga club championship. Dave Mowat records that she joined Tauranga in 1961, and the club’s honour roll lists her first senior title in 1974. These clues suggest the mid-1960s, with the exact issue still unconfirmed.
