1994 onwards

A new way to run the club

The move from a general committee to a Board introduced new responsibilities and a period of adjustment.

The new Board structure took effect in 1994. The first elected members were Bruce Partridge and Joyce Paris, joined by president Noel Copestake and players’ representative Paul Munn; Ian Cross and Jackie McDonald were invited to fill the remaining positions.

Bruce Partridge served as inaugural chair. The book describes the early work of defining the roles of Board, general manager and players’ committees, alongside different views about the new arrangements.

Later Boards worked on finance, course improvement and the clubhouse. Dave gives particular attention to the planning behind the redevelopment and to leadership documents prepared by Grant Scoones.

This is the book’s account of the transition, including its author’s assessments. The full administration chapter preserves the detail and names so members can read beyond this short introduction.
