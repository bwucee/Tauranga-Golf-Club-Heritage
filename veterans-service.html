1985–2014

The projects behind the pleasure

The Veterans’ contribution extended from friendly rounds to practical work around the course.

In 1985 the Veterans proposed undertaking projects agreed with the club in return for relief from course fees for their annual tournament. The book records the arrangement and lists the work carried out in the years that followed.

Projects ranged from painting, paths and ball washers to gardens, steps, signs and improvements to tees and greens. The year-by-year record shows how many small jobs accumulated into a substantial contribution.

The account also recognises the volunteers who maintained the Society’s album and the group associated with firewood and tree work. These were contributions in time and skill as well as in money.

The original Veterans’ chapter includes the full project lists, office holders and personalities. New Senior Men records can continue that pattern, while keeping the book’s historical account distinct from current activities.
