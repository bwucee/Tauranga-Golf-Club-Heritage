1950s

Opening the way for younger players

The book recalls a debate over schoolboys’ championship eligibility and an early competition for schoolgirls.

Dave Mowat records that Michael Cambie and Robert Mowat qualified for club championships during the 1950s but encountered objections because they were schoolboys paying junior subscriptions.

James Mowat and others supported their right to compete. According to the account, those arguments prevailed and the boys were allowed to participate. The recollection does not give an exact year, so this story remains dated to the decade.

The Ladies’ minutes record a different attempt to encourage younger players: Dr Jean Reading’s 1955 competition for school-age girls. It did not continue the following year because there was insufficient interest.

The photograph of Labour Weekend contenders around 1950 includes a barefoot caddie identified in the book as Brian Ducker. It shows another way younger people encountered the game. The boy in that image is not identified as either of the championship players discussed here.
