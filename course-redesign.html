1934 onwards

Beyond the racecourse oval

A redesign helped transform the early Gate Pa layout into a more settled golf course.

The original Gate Pa course extended into the racecourse oval. In its account of 1934, the book describes the club commissioning Auckland professional Alex Murray to advise on a new layout.

The review removed the five holes using the oval and changed other parts of the route. Members had also hoped to avoid the demanding climbs associated with The Drop and The Hummocks. Retained holes were renumbered around the new sequence.

The account links this change with a growing determination to move beyond makeshift playing conditions. References to present-day hole numbers in the book describe the course as it stood in 2014; they should not be assumed to describe every later alteration.

One date needs care: page 37 associates the 1934 changes with a new 33-year lease, while page 23 places the board’s authority to grant that lease term in 1944. This preview retains the stated redesign date but does not use the disputed lease detail to explain it. The original lease and course records would help settle that point.
