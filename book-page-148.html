The members were Richard Cashmore as Chairman, Anna Brabyn (President), Kevin Steward (Captain), Bruce Calderwood, Murray Goodhue, and Margot McCool. Now that a very successful clubhouse upgrade had been completed this Board turned its attention to the Pro Shop. This was seen to provide an important first impression to newcomers to the Club and its condition at the time was not in keeping with the standards the Board aspired to. Plans for the upgrade were prepared in 2004 and the new-look facility was completed in time for ‘Golf Fusion’ to move in during February 2006.

The new operation headed by Jack and Jay Carter introduced a new standard of service to members and visitors alike. ‘Golf Fusion’ was required to operate under a new agreement that required them to extend the role to one considerably wider than had previously been the case. This they have done with the result that the level of service provided to members and to visitors alike would surely not be exceeded elsewhere in this country.

The 2006 Board appointed Graeme Duncan, previously based at Inglewood, and a native of Scotland, to the vital post of Greens Superintendent. Graeme proved his worth quickly and the course continued to develop as it had done in recent years through the skill and application of men of the ilk of Brendan Allen and Murray Clark.

Richard Cashmore retired from the Board in 2007 and was replaced as Chairman by Murray Goodhue who unfortunately served for just that one year. Consequently the Board of 2009 had something of a new look about it with new members Richard Craven and Michael Smith joining Anna Brabyn, Bruce Calderwood, Murray Goodhue and Margot McCool.

Richard Craven was thrust into the Chairman’s role rather sooner than he may have anticipated when the 2010 elections saw Graham Cathie and Bob Diprose elected to join the President and Captain. These four invited Richard and Margot to continue in their roles. Richard stood down from the Chairmanship after just the one year so adding to the instability that is inevitable when management roles turn over too quickly. Following the 2011 AGM Graham Cathie was appointed to the chair after just the one year on the Board. Michelle Towersey and Evan McKain were invited to join the sitting members.

SECRETARIES AND MANAGERS

Any organisation whether commercial, charitable, public or private is very dependent upon one particular person to act as the hub upon which the business of the organisation revolves. In the first years of our Golf Club that person was the Secretary, a role filled by Mr John Bull until he was elected Captain. The job was then elevated to Honorary Secretary/Treasurer and filled by Mr G A Ward though the honorarium, if any, must have been very small based on the level of expenses quoted in the BOP Times of the day. Perhaps Mr H B Mountfort, who was said to chase about the Township to collect subscriptions in order to be able to pay to have the Gate Pa Links mown ready for the new season, was the first to really get the Club established on a more secure financial basis.
