1974–2014

When the day’s work was done

Twilight golf brought together summer evenings, volunteers and a different route into the club.

Twilight tournaments began in the summer of 1974–75. The events used ten holes because the tenth finished near the clubhouse, making a shared meal a natural part of the evening.

Nen Sim and a team of volunteers organised early catering and kept careful records. Later, Noel Copestake and others developed a programme able to handle much larger fields, with several starting routes and their own cards.

Business House golf grew under Gary Baker’s encouragement, bringing teams and players without club membership into the evening competition. The chapter describes both its social appeal and its logistical demands.

Dave also records the development of a twilight handicap system and its eventual incorporation into the club’s computer software. The system described is historical; current conditions of competition belong on the operational club website.
