GLOSSARY Weasel - A shot holed from off the green.

Nearie - Nearest the pin on a par three provided the ball is on the green. Grittie - Par following a bunker stroke. Fannie - A tee stroke failing to reach the Women’s tee. Jesney – Nearies are carried forward if no one is on the green with their tee shot. If these have not been settled by the time the final hole is played the nearest the pin after three shots on the 18th wins the accumulated stars.

An extension to the Ball in the Bucket Brigade evolved as the ‘Baker’s Dozen’. A small group from within the regular Thursday players decided to make an out of town trip to enjoy courses they had not played. The first such trip was planned for Napier in 1991. Gary Baker was supposed to be one of the touring group but had to pull out at the last moment. Though there were never a dozen Gary was somehow thought of as the extra loaf and so the name came into being. Ten players made that initial trip, travelling together in a mini-van and staying at Ormly Lodge alongside the Napier Golf Club course at Maraenui.

Since that initial adventure trips have been organised each year. The destinations have included the Manawatu, Dunedin, Christchurch, and even the Gold Coast. The travelling party eventually reached a head count of 32 and many the tales that are told, or often not told, of these memorable tours. Those intending to travel make voluntary weekly contributions to the pool, while a three putt results in a compulsory contribution.

In the middle years of the first decade of the new Century another Thursday group became established. Utilising the same ‘ball in the bucket’ draw technology this group assembles prior to 8am and tees off from the first and seventh tees. The entry fee at time of writing stands at $2, the stakes are set at one, one, two, and first prize is a golf ball and a small chocolate bar. Depending on the number of entries the next several accumulators of Stableford points receive one golf ball each. The group early voted to

not reward twos in order to allow for more balls to be available to those scoring good Stableford scores. Naturally a number of regulars recall scoring a two the week following their vote being recorded against rewarding this particular feat.
