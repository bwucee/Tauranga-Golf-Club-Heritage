ACKNOWLEDGING THE FOUNDATION

From Dave’s book to a growing collection.

An extension of the work behind Pars from the Past, with space for the club’s continuing story.

Years of work, brought together

Edited by David G. S. Mowat and published in 2014, Pars from the Past marked one hundred years of golf on the Gate Pa links. Dave brought together research, recollections, photographs and records to create a lasting account of the club.

The book acknowledges the earlier historical work of Joan Mirrielees and Deidre Hartley, as well as the many members and contributors who helped preserve this material. This website carries those acknowledgements forward.

A foundation we can build on

The initial historical stories are drawn from the book, with page references retained. As more material is added, its own sources and contributors will be identified. New contributions will remain distinguishable from the book’s account.

History and the life of the club

The historical framework gives visitors a way through time. Club group collections will also preserve more recent achievements, activities, photographs and records, so today’s club life can become part of tomorrow’s history.

Visitors can start with a short overview and choose how far to follow a subject. The structure is in place; the detail will grow a piece at a time.

Book context: Pars from the Past (2014), title page, acknowledgements, foreword and introduction, pp. 1–5.
