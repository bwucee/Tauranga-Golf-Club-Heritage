Veteran men’s golf

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

← Previous pageBook p. 131 / 305Next page →
From the book: original views, recollections and terminology are retained. References to “today”, rules, staff or programmes describe the time of writing. Text extraction is being reviewed; use the page reference when suggesting a correction.

In 1996 Ron Holden was President of the Society.

• One major project highlighted Ron’s year. This was the construction of a Caddie Car shed abutted to the Greenkeeper’s building beside the practise range. The Society provided the materials, then designed and constructed the building. • On-going activities included the maintenance projects mentioned earlier.

In 1997 under President Ted Otway the Society: • Painted the Course Managers Staff facility. • Painted the distance marker blocks. • Repaired paving at the first tee. • Repaired the path to 5th tee with bitumen seal. • Laid rubber matting on the above path. • Fixed new Colour Steel cladding, gifted by Longrun Industries, to the inside of the Clubhouse fascia. • Realigned and relaid a path from 17th green to 18th tee, including new timber railing. • Poured concrete storm water channel beside 18th tee. • Poured concrete to form a storm water catchment off the car park. Redrilled the existing soak hole to a depth of 8m. • Installed irrigation to serve the shrubbery on the path to the 18th tee and to the bank of the 2nd tee. • Purchased a new fairway irrigator and associated hoses. • Fabricated a 1.8m wide steel roller for use behind the greens mower.

In 1998 under President Matt Sproule the Society: • Laid a storm water channel along the pathway over the causeway. • Resurfaced paths on the 4th, 5th, and 11th holes. • Arranged and financed drilling and piping of soak holes at a number of locations. • Financed and constructed the erection of pipe railing beside the roadway past the10th green.

In 1999 under President Joe Loughlin the Society:

• Completed projects commenced in 1998 including the design and installation of a sliding roof over the sand-storage bin in the implement shed area. • Maintained the car park lines and garden areas.

In 2000 under President George Riley the Society:

• Donated $6000 to be used to finance an upgrade of the path at the 1st tee and that from the 3rd green to the 4th tee. • Purchased and installed a compressor and air pressure cylinder in the implement shed. • Installed piping to wash down area for golf carts. • Maintained paint work on pipe fencing and car park lines. • Contributed funds to Ladies’ shrub planting project.
