1963–1995

Water: the work beneath the fairways

A reliable water supply took decades of investigation, investment and practical adjustment.

The earliest system drew from the Borough supply, with sprinklers that needed shifting by hand. Some greens were beyond the pipe network, and water pressure depended on demands elsewhere in the town.

The book records investigating the nearby stream in 1963 and a bore in 1970. A reservoir and further pipework followed, but keeping enough water available remained a difficulty.

In 1978 the club approved a more extensive reticulation system. By the 1990s its limitations had prompted another major decision: a professionally designed replacement, completed in April 1995.

The chapter explains both the engineering and the work of staff who moved sprinklers, often at night. Its technical descriptions and water-right references describe the position when the book was written, not current operating instructions.
