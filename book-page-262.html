Professional golf returns to Tauranga

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

← Previous pageBook p. 262 / 305Next page →

Grant Moorhead of Eisenhower Trophy fame moved into second place with an excellent 66. Josh Geary, not especially comfortable with his game, still managed to shoot 68-67 to share fourth place. Local New Zealand representative Jared Pender improved five strokes on his opening round of 71 to make the top ten. After three rounds Hendry, Moorhead, and Brad Shilton led by one stroke from Geary with many others poised to make a serious challenge for the title. A brilliant final round 64 looked to have ensured Geary of his first victory as a Professional as others fell by the wayside. Eventually only Moorhead looked to have a chance to match Geary’s 14 under total.

This possibility increased dramatically when he holed a 5m putt on the tough par four 17th, a hole where Geary earlier had dropped his only stroke of the round after his second had ended with the ball wrapped in a fallen branch to the left of the green. Courageously Josh had elected to play the ball as it lay rather than pay the penalty for deeming it unplayable. The two-stroke swing on this penultimate hole sent the pair into a play-off when Grant failed to birdie the final hole. In deference to the large crowd estimated at fifteen hundred that clustered about the 18th green the players returned to that tee to settle the issue.

Both missed birdie opportunities as had been the case when playing the hole as the 72nd so back to the tee they went. This time Geary thrilled the crowd by firing his wedge second to within 30cms of the hole to ensure victory.

Fellow local Jared Pender shot the low round of the tournament, a brilliant 63, to elevate himself to fourth place overall and leading Amateur one stroke clear of Gary John Hill. This showing may well have cemented Jared’s claim to a spot on the Eisenhower team.

THE 2009 CARRUS TAURANGA OPEN CHAMPIONSHIP

Peter Fowler, the Auckland based Australian Professional, may well have been the favourite to win the 2009 Carrus Tauranga Open and his opening 65 did nothing to dent public confidence. Blair Shaw,

of Hastings, matched Fowler’s score while another eight players lay within two strokes of the lead. These included local Amateur Brad Kendall of Mt Maunganui and Jared Pender, now a member of the Professional ranks. Shaw’s round featured an ace on the difficult par three fifth hole, the ball pitching just 15cms from the cup before finding the bottom of the hole.

Fowler scored a second 65 to open up a three-stroke lead over Pender whose 66 left him one stroke clear of three other rookie Professionals in Troy Ropiha, Blair Shaw, and Hamish Robertson. Cecilia Cho followed her opening 72 with a brilliant round of 67 to take a decisive lead into the final round of the Women’s section.

Peter Fowler carried his wonderful form into the third with an excellent 66 to leave him in a seemingly impregnable position three strokes clear of his nearest pursuer. Michael Hendry and Hamish Robertson shot 65s to inch closer to that lead, while Pender’s nine under total suggested a high finish was well within his grasp on his home course.
