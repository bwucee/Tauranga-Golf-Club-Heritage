1994 This year the tournament was designated as an official trial for both the Bay of Plenty/Thames Valley and Waikato/King Country teams thus ensuring all prospective team members in those regions were entered. Top seed and recently crowned World Left Handed Champion Lisa Aldridge staved off the challenge of the Northern stars, defeating Brenda Ormsby in the final.

1995 This year saw the emergence of Tina Howard of Taupo who was to have a significant presence in the Una Wickham Championship over the following years. Barely qualifying for the Championship flight in 14th spot the teenager defeated Ingrid van Steenbergen, Sandra Carey, and Robyn Pellow (Ngaruawahia) on her way to meeting the 1992 champion Gina Scott in the final. In that match Howard surged to a five-hole lead after seven holes and looked certain to bring off a huge upset when still four up with just five to play. It has long been claimed that whoever wins the 14th at Tauranga wins the match and Scott must have been aware of this as she birdied the par three to start her come-back.

Pars over the testing 15th, 16th, and 17th holes and a birdie three at the 18th brought Scott level before she won the title with a further birdie at the first extra hole. After dropping seven strokes over the first six holes Scott was level with par over the final twelve.

Thirteen year old Julia Kranschinski distinguished herself by reaching the semi-finals where she bowed to the ultimate champion by 3/ 2.

1996 The Auckland, Waikato, and Bay of Plenty selectors all designated the tournament this year as an official trial again ensuring a quality field faced the starter. Tina Howard defeated Joanne Croskery, Catherine Knight, and Annette Stott on the way to another showdown against Gina Scott in the final. Gina had made it to the final by defeating three members of the BOP/TV representative team in Joanne Henderson, Julia Kraschinski, and Brenda Ormsby. As in the 1995 final between these two Tina began with a rush to be five up after just eight holes and though she three putted the ninth she still made the turn one under the card and four up.

Unlike in the previous year she did not allow Gina back into the match and won comfortably.

1997 Kaye Carroll returned to the tournament after an absence of twelve years during which she

produced a family of four children. Kaye (nee Johnson) had been an unknown teenage golfer from Opotiki when Una Wickham, realising the potential of the youngster, plucked her from comparative obscurity to join her in the Bay of Plenty team. Together they enjoyed a number of Russell Grace successes. Thus it was not difficult to see why Kaye had long harboured an ambition to win the Una Wickham Memorial since her first appearance in the event. In the final Kaye Carroll defeated Wendy Hawkes one up after an exciting match. On the way Carroll had defeated ex Tauranga player Joanne Brooks (Grange) in round one and Eleanor Hodgson (New Plymouth) in the Semi-final.
