Faced with a shortage of volunteers to perform this final task Manager Grant Scoones contacted local Backpacker Hostels and soon young people from many Countries including Austria, Argentina, Belgium, England, Scotland, Germany, and Italy joined the working group. These young men and women were keen to earn some cash and proved to be willing and diligent workers with the result that the job was completed within three days. While this method proved successful, and much was learnt that might have enabled subsequent plantings to be carried out more efficiently, there were obvious drawbacks. The turf alone cost $7000 while the labour requirement was very high.

In view of these factors it was decided to continue the establishment program using our own material obtained by grooving our existing fairways. Unfortunately this has meant the program has progressed at the rate of just one fairway per year. At least this minimised the inconvenience to members caused by taking each treated fairway out of play for several months. The eighth and tenth fairways were successfully converted to Windsor Green in 2008 and 2009 respectively with minimum disruption to play as staff became more adept at handling this new technique. The program lapsed in 2010 before resuming in 2011 when a successful conversion of the 18th fairway was achieved.

At this stage there was still much to be done to convert the remaining fairways.
