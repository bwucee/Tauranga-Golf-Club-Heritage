1953–2014

The people behind the professional’s counter

Teaching, equipment, encouragement and the running of a business all formed part of the professional’s role.

The book follows the resident professionals from James Mowat to Jim Paterson, Greg Holst, Allan Snape, Rick Barker and the later shop team. Each worked with the facilities and circumstances of a different period.

Jim Paterson’s teaching style and ball striking receive close attention. Later profiles describe professional experience, business changes and the personal relationships that developed with members.

The 1986 fire in the shop and trundler shed was a major interruption during Allan Snape’s time. The account records the loss of stock and members’ equipment and the club’s response to help the family.

Rick Barker’s work with beginners connects directly to the development of nine-hole golf. Jay Carter’s story connects junior golf, professional training and the continuing service of the shop. All the profiles can be read in their original chapter.
