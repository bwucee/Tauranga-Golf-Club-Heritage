PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Keith Frith

On his daughter’s wedding day in 1968, Keith Frith’s friend Vince O’Connor talked him into playing the second round of the Nicoll Cup at dawn. Keith finished the competition 12 up against par over 36 holes. Members also knew him for seven consecutive years as captain and for coming back to fill the role when the club needed him again.
