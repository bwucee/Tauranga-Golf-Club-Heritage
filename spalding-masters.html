1968–1972

The stars come to Gate Pa

The Spalding Masters brought international visitors, large galleries and remarkable scoring to the course.

The book records the Spalding approach in 1967 and the first tournament in January 1968. The event gave Tauranga a place on the growing summer circuit.

Billy Dunk won the inaugural event, while scoring and course-record debates became part of the story. In 1969 Bob Charles produced a total of 260, with rounds that included 62 and 63.

The chapter follows the changing fields and contests over successive years, including Stuart Jones’s challenge to the professionals. It records the spectators and atmosphere as well as the winning totals.

The original year-by-year account continues into the NZPGA events staged at Tauranga. Its details can be explored by page, keeping the short overview separate from the full tournament narrative.
