1974–1980

National championship days

Tauranga’s NZPGA championships brought leading professionals back to the club.

Tauranga first staged the NZPGA championship in January 1974 after the Mount Maunganui greens required rebuilding. The book explains that connection before turning to the tournament itself.

The later accounts include a dramatic 1977 finish between John Lister and Allan Brask. Lister drew level late and won the play-off, with spectators crowding around the closing holes.

In 1978 Simon Owen emerged as winner after Guy Wolstenholme lost ground at the finish. The 1980 account returns to Bob Charles and the pressure of tournament putting.

These are fuller stories than a list of champions can convey. The original chapter retains the rounds, turning points, comments and local connections for readers who want to follow them.
