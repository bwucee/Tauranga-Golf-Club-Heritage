The women veterans’ golf club

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

← Previous pageBook p. 135 / 305Next page →

This organization was launched in 1987 under the enthusiastic instigation of Jean Mack and Phyl Carter, the latter being recognised as the first president. Phyl and her husband Reg were wonderful Club members who both participated fully in Club activities over many years. When the undulations of our course became rather too much for Reg in his latter years they became members of the Omokoroa Club where they again participated very fully. Their home was adjacent to the entrance of that Club and Phyl took on the role of caterer to the Club for a number of years during which she provided a magnificent service.

Tauranga women members who were actively involved with Phyl in establishing the fledgling Lady Veterans’ Group included Hazel Casey, Marg Calver, Mona Lilly, Kath Christie, Joan Standford, Shirley Morris, and Shirley Crawford. This group were ultimately elected to form the committee when the inaugural AGM was held in November 1988, Marg Calver serving as the first elected President.

At that first meeting it was decided to request that members should make a $2 donation each year to maintain membership. A further donation of $1 was requested so that a presentation could be made to the caterer Mary Goodjohn who gave up her day-off in order to cater to the group on the one Monday each month when the veteran women held their competition day. Mary was most gratified to receive a kitchen slicer from the proceeds of this collection. Hardly a personal gift but very practical as the kitchen was, at that time, ill equipped for the functions it served.

In 1989 a tradition was established whereby members would travel by bus to Okoroire during the October coring week in order to enjoy a round of golf followed by a group luncheon at the hotel there. Twenty-nine ladies made that first trip and enjoyed a wonderful day out. The costs were interesting. The bus cost $8 per head, green fees $5, while for lunch there was a choice of an $11 buffet or a three course meal for $15.

From those early years it was established that the AGM would be held in November and take the form of a Christmas lunch preceding the meeting. Fifty-two members attended the AGM of 1989.

In July 1990 ten veteran ladies from each of the Omokoroa and Mt. Maunganui clubs were invited to participate in the monthly competition so beginning a tradition of Inter-club visitations. In November 1990 another step was made in the direction of establishing relationships with other clubs when an open Christmas Hamper Tournament was staged in association with the BOP/TV Lady Vets Association. These tournaments were organised into five zones within the District with Te Puke, Omanu, Mt. Maunganui, Otumoetai, and Tauranga members being eligible to compete in the Tauranga event. The Veterans negotiated a reduction of $100 on the $500 fee normally charged for the use of the course. One hundred and five players participated in that first event.

At this stage in the history of the Lady Vets no subscription had been required beyond the initial $2 donation upon joining. Despite this the club had been able to build a surplus as the result of raffles, the Okoroire trip, the Christmas function, and other activities.
