Imagined future · written in 2014

Dave looks a hundred years ahead

The book closes with a creative look forward, written as if a future historian were looking back.

Dave explicitly introduces the closing chapter as a prediction of how the club and course might develop over the next century. Much of it is written in the past tense from an imagined future viewpoint.

He considers climate, water, turf, equipment, course changes, the economy and the future appeal of golf. These passages reveal the concerns and hopes of the author at the time of writing.

They are not evidence that the predicted changes happened. The dates and developments described after the short-course section have therefore been excluded from the website’s historical timeline.

The full ending remains available as part of the book, labelled as an imagined future. Preserving that distinction honours the form Dave chose without turning his speculation into a historical claim.
