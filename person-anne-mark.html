PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Anne Mark

Anne Mark carried her experience at Tauranga into national golf administration. In 1960, as a member of the Ladies’ Golf Union executive, she sent the club news of a change to scorecard requirements. Members also knew her as a player and team manager, one of several ways she helped shape women’s golf both at home and beyond the club.
