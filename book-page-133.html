Veteran men’s golf

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

In 2010 under President Ross Fletcher the Society: • provided finance for the connection of the water supply from Cameron Road to the fountain at the 13th green. • Contributed $4000 towards the establishment of the turf nursery developed beyond the third fairway. • Veteran volunteers helped in laying Windsor Green stolons on the 18th fairway and the turf nursery – a full day project.

Cliff Horsburgh was playing the eleventh hole one day in the company of a gentleman who was claimed to have been the Captain of a Club in the Manawatu. Cliff’s tee stroke was seen to ricochet from the roof of the stable out onto the fairway. As Cliff set off down the path towards it the visitor enquired if perhaps he should play another. Upon Cliff asking why he would do that he was informed his ball was out of bounds. “No. It is in the fairway” protested Cliff. “Yes but it has struck something out of bounds so that is what counts” was the response. After Cliff gained support from the other two in the group the visitor grudgingly conceded the point with the statement: “Well, that is not how it would be handled at our Club”.

Ray Pittams Ray heads the list of those members who have achieved the feat of playing to or below their age on a number of occasions. While other multiple winners include Pat Kendall, Clive Sheerin and Cliff Breeds, none approach Ray’s record. During the Spring of 2013 Ray played his fiftieth such round and followed that up within days with his fifty first. Ray retired from farming in the Waipa Valley of the King Country and moved to Tauranga in 1977. Having been a regular visitor to our Labour weekend tournament Ray had a soft spot for the Club and sought membership only to be told membership was closed to all but those boasting a National Handicap.

Consequently Ray joined Omanu and was admitted to Tauranga as a restricted B category member in 1980 before finally gaining full membership. Ray first played to his age at the age of 71 in 1996 and has achieved the feat each year since with the exception of 1999 when he was 74. Ray became a valuable member of our Intermediate Pennant team and featured in the winning team of 1998. To cap his membership at Tauranga he won the Intermediate Championship in 2012.
