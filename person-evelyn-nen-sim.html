PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Evelyn (Nen) Sim

In 1974, Nen Sim became the first woman elected to the club executive. That same summer she led the twilight catering volunteers. Her records show them feeding 70 people on one January evening in 1975, serving roast chicken, corned beef and salads for a dollar a head. The proceeds later helped equip the clubhouse kitchen.
