This proved difficult to administer in the pre-computer era and for some years it was contested as a Match Play event open to all members whatever their handicap. This led to some interesting singles matches involving senior players having to give a stroke a hole, and sometimes more. That is very difficult to do. The Mowat family provided vouchers that the recipients could use to subsidise purchases of their own choice. The sight of Reg Carter one year proudly donning a Club jacket that he had decided was the ideal prize to commemorate his win lives vividly in the memory.

Following a period when it was awarded for the best aggregate nett score over the Stroke Play Championships the trophy reverted to its original role, computers having now made record keeping much simpler.

The Pinto Fruit Juice Company donated The Pinto Cup for Foursomes competition. Past president Dan Dougherty was manager of the company at a time when the company was a major supplier to the Club.

Other important trophies are the Alan Bradmore Memorial Cup and the Nicoll Cup. Harry Bradmore presented the former in memory of his son Alan who was killed in the Second World War. It is contested over two rounds of Stableford competition. Harry and his son Maurice have been acknowledged earlier in relation to their invaluable work in relation to the trees on the course. Life Member Warwick Nicoll presented the latter Cup and it is contested over two rounds of Par competition. WOMEN’S TROPHIES Many women and some men have donated trophies that are contested throughout the year by our women players. Although some of these trophies, such as those related to the Una Wickham Tournament, are no longer contested there remain a considerable number that are keenly contested each year.

Trophies associated with the Una Wickham Memorial Tournament include: • The Una Wickham Memorial Salver presented to the Championship winner. This trophy was presented to the club by Una’s brother Neville, his wife, and her aunt Mrs Rennie Wallace (Gordon). • The KE Carter Motors Salver (Second 16). Ken Carter was an excellent player who represented Wellington prior to his move to Tauranga.

Trophies associated with the Open Tournament in May include:

• The Fairway Cup donated by Nancy Hare, a former Captain and President and awarded to the Tauranga player returning the best Gross score. •The Nancy Burne Trophy donated by past Lady President Nancy and awarded to the Tauranga player returning the best Nett score. • The Hazel Casey Salver awarded to the Tauranga player in the Bronze division returning the best Nett score. • The Moana McAllister Salver awarded to the Tauranga player returning the best Stableford score. Moana was a member of the Waihi Club and a regular visitor to our tournaments.
