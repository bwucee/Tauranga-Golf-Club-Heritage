1951–1954

Building for a bigger club

Members helped finance a new clubhouse as postwar growth outstripped the old building.

The history records the sale of four hundred £10 debentures to help finance a new brick clubhouse. Alan Davidson, Dave Cambie and Lionel Smith formed the building committee.

The new building opened in 1954, on the site used by the later clubhouse. Photographs on page 55 show two views of the building, with the putting area and open course beside it.

The old wooden clubhouse was sold and relocated to Pyes Pa. The choice of the new building’s position had prompted debate, a reminder that even a widely welcomed improvement could raise different views among members.

The financial figures need verification: page 53 describes a construction ceiling of £5,700 and completion below budget; the later committee summary gives a cost of £5,750. Those passages may use different cost bases, but the book does not settle that question. The debenture record and opening year are presented separately from the unresolved final cost.
