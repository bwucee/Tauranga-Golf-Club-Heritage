From the 1968 programme

1968 · PROGRAMME

Preparing Gate Pa: Silvester and Butler

A contemporary account and photograph of Grant Silvester and Wally Butler preparing the course, including Silvester’s assessment of Commander Harris.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Jim Paterson and the professional shop

A portrait, professional-shop advertisement and tournament field entry place Jim Paterson within the 1968 club.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Visiting players in the 1968 programme

Named photographs include Walter Godfrey, Guy Wolstenholme, Martin Roesink, Billy Dunk, Brian Boys, Frank Malloy and other competitors.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Mrs K. Johnston: a national champion

The programme reports her victory in the preceding August at the New Zealand Māori Ladies’ Championship. Its Johnston spelling differs from Johnstone in the book.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Frank Macklow: golf, cricket and hockey

A portrait and profile provide sporting and personal context for Macklow’s veterans achievements.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Noel Hockin and the L. C. Smith Rose Bowl

A profile of regional and club service. The January 1968 programme presents Hockin as still serving, whereas the book gives a 1967 retirement.

1968 · PROGRAMME

Professionals at the earlier Rotorua Masters

The named group photograph reproduced in the 1968 programme is from the earlier Rotorua event, not Tauranga’s 1968 field.
