PEOPLE & CLUB LIFE

Warwick Nicol

Warwick Nicol worked as a bank accountant in Tauranga and spent many years as the club’s men’s handicapper. He was already serving as president of the Bay of Plenty District Golf Association when he took up the Tauranga presidency in 1958. The club made him a Life Member that year, before his retirement to Tirau.
