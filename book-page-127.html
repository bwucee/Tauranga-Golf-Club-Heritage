Veteran men’s golf

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

← Previous pageBook p. 127 / 305Next page →
From the book: original views, recollections and terminology are retained. References to “today”, rules, staff or programmes describe the time of writing. Text extraction is being reviewed; use the page reference when suggesting a correction.

Up until this Jubilee year Veterans have been accepted from age 55 but in an endeavour to contain our numbers to a manageable level that limit has now been raised to 60 years. Our roll now stands at 130 plus. All the trophies presented years ago by some of the founding members are still regular features of our annual programme. A notable addition to the trophy list came in 1976 when Colin Cathie gave the Society the Cornelius Cup, named for his very successful racehorse of that name. This fine cup is contested as a Match Play event with the final being held on Club Finals Day. This has become recognised as the major award contested by our members over the season.

In addition to presenting the cup Colin arranged for a debenture, the annual interest from which would provide funds for a personal prize to be awarded the winner. This was a most pleasing and generous gesture by Colin.

A notable achievement of recent times was the feat of one of our members, Bill Johns, to hold a single figure handicap for 50 consecutive years. Bill, or “Dummy “ Johns as he has been affectionately known for many years was honoured by the Tauranga Golf Club with the presentation of a silver salver to commemorate this remarkable feat, most of which was achieved prior to his moving to Tauranga.

Cliff Colson, President of our Society in 1974, has done an enormous amount of work for Veterans’ golf over recent years. He has been a committeeman, Treasurer, Secretary, and President. His work has been recognised by his current appointment as Vice President of the NZ Veterans’ Association. It is

hoped that this will lead to the Presidency in 1978.

The story of our Society has so far been one of men who have been prepared to give generously of their time, their talents, and their material resources. As the Society has grown it has required a high level of organisational expertise and we have never lacked for volunteers with the necessary skills. It has not been possible to cover all aspects of our history in this publication, nor to pay personal tribute to all those many people whose ideas and practical assistance have contributed so much

to the success of the Society over the past 25 years. What has been done has been achieved in a wonderful spirit of friendship and camaraderie that would be hard to match elsewhere. The solid base that has been established over the first 25 years augurs well for the years ahead. We hope that in telling this story we have rekindled some of the wonderful spirit of the past so that the ideals of our founders may burn brightly into the future.

So ended the History of the first 25 years of organised Veteran Golf in Tauranga as compiled by Bill Sanders and Ken McIntyre.

THE SUCCEEDING YEARS

When Bernie Baker became President of the Society in 1993 he inherited a number of papers relating to past happenings within the Society. These included a copy of the above history of the first 25 years and, upon reading it, Bernie was prompted to initiate the production of an album to record the Officers and activities of the Society on a year-by-year basis. This album commenced in 1974 as this was the earliest year for which full committee details could be discovered.
