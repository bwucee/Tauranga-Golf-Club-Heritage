Sadly Deidre’s health began to deteriorate before she was able to achieve as much as she would have wished in furthering the evolution of this history. Subsequently I was presented with the material that had been assembled to that date in the hope that I could use it to put together a coherent record of the first one hundred years of golf in Tauranga.

I soon realised that a great deal remained to be done. For example nothing had been done to document the deeds of the many outstanding visiting golfers who had competed in tournaments, professional and amateur, male and female, on our course. World-class performers, including winners of ‘Majors’ such as the British and US Opens, had graced our links and deserved their place in our history. The supposed Centennial in 2011 was rapidly approaching and a great deal of material still had to be collected, analysed, and organised into coherent form. Fortunately the deadline was extended forward by three years when further research proved the Club dated back to 1904.

A great many people have contributed richly to the development of the Tauranga Golf Club and I have endeavoured to give them credit for that. Unfortunately this could only be done from memory and from the meagre documentation that does exist. There will be many unsung heroes whom I have not been able to acknowledge and to them I apologise. They will have had the deep satisfaction of knowing that they helped develop and improve a vital asset to pass on to future generations. To those stalwarts of the past that would have been reward enough. My grateful thanks go to all those who have made a contribution to this history by way of oral or written material, by donating photographs, or by collecting information.

Without them it would have been impossible to produce more than a basic outline of our colourful history.

Finally, though I have endeavoured to report facts in an impartial manner, it has been impossible to refrain from allowing some personal opinion to creep into this narrative. For that I make no apology. The relationship between the Mowat family and the Tauranga Golf Club since my father took up the role of Professional in 1936 dictates that it could never be otherwise.
