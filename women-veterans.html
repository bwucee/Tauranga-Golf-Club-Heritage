1987–2014

The women veterans find their own rhythm

Monthly golf, visits and shared lunches built another enduring part of club life.

The book traces the women veterans’ group to the initiative of Jean Mack and Phyl Carter in 1987. At the first formal annual meeting in November 1988, Marg Calver was elected president.

Competition was only part of the attraction. Bus trips, lunches, visits with other clubs and Christmas gatherings helped establish the group’s character. Members also supported improvements to club facilities.

Nine-hole players joined the monthly competitions by 1999. The chapter records how the programme adapted as both nine-hole and eighteen-hole membership grew.

The original chapter retains the names of organisers and the sequence of events through the period covered by the book. It gives this group a clear place within the Ladies collection without assuming that its 2014 programme describes today’s arrangements.
