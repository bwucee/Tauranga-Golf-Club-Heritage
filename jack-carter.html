A MEMBER’S OWN ACCOUNT

Jack Carter’s golfing life

From cleaning members’ clubs as a twelve-year-old in 1957 to junior golf, provincial teams and life membership in 2019: Jack Carter tells his own story.

By Jack Carter · Undated personal account supplied 10 November 2026. The reference to 46 years reflects the time of writing.

Jack’s recollections are presented in his own words. His account of membership and handicap arrangements describes his experience at the time, rather than a current club policy. Mowat also records Jack’s administrative service and his work with Jay in the pro shop on p. 78.

My first experience with golf was working for Jim Paterson, he was the professional at the time at Tauranga Golf Club. It was 1957 and I was 12 years old. My job was cleaning and buffing the members clubs after each round and storing them away in the sheds. My spare time was spent chipping and putting, while waiting for the members to finish their rounds.

In 1968 my wife Judy and I headed to Canada, and my job allowed me to play golf most days, joining a municipal golf club with two courses, where I received my first handicap of 12. We returned to New Zealand four years later. As Tauranga Golf Club had a closed membership, for the next 3 years I played my golf at Mount Maunganui Golf Club.

New Zealand Golf had a national handicap of 4, which allowed you to join any club in New Zealand.

Playing off a 5 handicap at Mount Maunganui, I worked diligently to obtain the handicap which allowed me to join the Tauranga Golf Club.

After a few years at Tauranga, I joined the committee under the guidance of Maurice Coad, Maurice White, Noel Copestake and Dan Dougherty. This Committee covered all aspects of the club; financials, course development, member groups (male and female), Juniors and Vets. Maurice Coad thought I'd be a good junior convener and asked if I would run the juniors on Sundays, and with New Zealand winning the Eisenhower Golf Trophy in Canada in 1992, junior golf had exploded in NZ, so I said yes.

In the mid 90's after the end of the Spalding Professional Golf Tournament I acquired the Spalding Cup and promoted the Spalding Masters Junior Under 19 mixed golf tournament - 72 holes played over 3 days. Some of the notable people in the fields included the likes of Lydia Ko, Ryan Fox, Josh Geary, Danny Lee, and my son, Jay Carter, who represented NZ at the Junior World Championships in Japan.

My personal achievements at the Tauranga Golf Club were winning the Senior Men's Championship Plate, the Men's Intermediate and Junior Championships and the other memorable feat was shooting a score of 64 off an 8 handicap in a Saturday competition.

Continuing on, I was involved with the BOP Golf alongside Chris McApline as CEO. I managed the Senior Men's Team which saw us travelling from Whangarei to Invercargill and winning three NZ Interprovincial Titles. I then went from Manager to Selector, a position I held for 3 years. Then, with the purchase of a Motorhome, I decided to slow down a bit and enjoy touring New Zealand.

My last committee involvement was three years on the Senior Men's (Vets) Committee.

In 2019 | was made a life member of the Tauranga Golf Club.

I have thoroughly enjoyed my 46 years involvement with the Tauranga Golf Club and all its wonderful members.

Editorial correction: Two occurrences of Maurice Code have been corrected to Maurice Coad at Bruce Partridge’s request on 10 November 2026. Other wording is retained as supplied.
