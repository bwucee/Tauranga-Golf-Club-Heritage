Honours, office holders and trophies

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

Trophies associated with Match Play competition include: • The Contenders Trophy donated by Anne Mark. Awarded to winner of Handicap Match Play for the top sixteen. • The Contenders Plate, donated by Shirley Eaton, a past Captain and President. • The Lillian Baker Cup, a handicap Match Play event for players aged sixty years or more. (Lillian, a Gate Pa identity was closely related to the writer Ngaio Marsh). • The Elsie Hanna Cup for handicap Match Play. • The Jean Cullen Consolation Cup contested by the second 32 following qualifying for the Elsie Hanna Cup. Jean was a former Ladies’ President. • The Coates Trophy for handicap Match Play.

Donated by Jackie and David Coates. • the Olga Johnson Trophy for Fourball Match Play – Senior division. • the Massey Salver for Intermediate Fourball Match Play. Pat Massey was a former Club Secretary. • the Eaton Trophy for Junior Fourball Match Play. Donated by Shirley Eaton, a past Ladies’ President.

Trophies associated with the Match Play Championships include: • The Sare Cup donated by Dawn Sare and awarded to the top qualifier in the Senior Championship. Dawn was Ladies Secretary at one time. • The Bongard Trophy awarded to the top qualifier in the Intermediate Championship. Jean Bongard was a past Ladies’ President. • The Sim Trophy donated by Nen Sim and awarded to the top qualifier in the Junior Championship. • The Anderson Rosebowl awarded to the best Nett qualifier in the Senior Championship. Robyn Anderson was a previous Ladies’ President. • The Audrey Payne Memorial Plate for the best nett qualifier in the Intermediate Championship. Donated by Noel Payne in memory of his wife. • The Alison Holdem Salver awarded to the best nett qualifier in the Junior Championship.

Alison served as Ladies’ Captain and President. • The Minie Jordan Salver awarded to the Senior Champion. • The Intermediate Cup awarded to the Intermediate Champion. • The Armstrong Cup awarded to the Junior Match Play Champion.

• The Hutcheson Trophy awarded to the Junior Girls’ Champion. Donated by Loeta Hutcheson who served terms as Captain and President. • The Ramsden Cup donated by Joan Ramsden for C Grade Handicap Match Play. Joan served a term as Ladies’ Secretary. • The Evans Plate donated by Elva Evans and awarded to the Senior Championship Runner Up. • The Davidson Salver donated by Fay Davidson and awarded to the Intermediate Championship Runner Up.
