Honours, office holders and trophies

David G. S. Mowat, editor · Published 2014

• The Wagstaff Salver donated by Patsy Wagstaff and awarded to the Junior Championship Runner Up. • The Committee Salver awarded to the Junior Girls’ Championship Runner Up. • The Una Wickham Plate donated by Miss Wickham and awarded to the winner of the Senior Championship Plate. • The Betty Gordon Salver donated by Mrs Gordon and awarded to the Intermediate Championship Plate winner. • The McEwan Trophy donated by Pam McEwan and awarded to the Junior Championship Plate winner • The Margaret Mowat Trophy donated by Mrs Mowat for the second 16 in the Senior division. • The Edwards Trophy donated by Joyce Edwards for the second 16 in the Intermediate division. • Mrs Bradmores’ Cup donated by Mrs Harry Bradmore for the second 16 in the Junior division.

Mrs Bradmore was a generous donor to the Ladies’ section of the Club.

Tropies associated with the Stroke Play Championships include: • The Nelle Golden Plate awarded to the player scoring the best Nett in the first round. Ken Golden presented this trophy in memory of his wife. • The Dorothy Garland Trophy is awarded to the player recording the best aggregate Nett score over the rounds of the Championship. Dorothy served as Captain in the late Seventies after she and Tom moved to Tauranga from Reporoa. • The Loeta Hutcheson Trophy is awarded to the Senior Stroke Play Champion. • The Cross Trophy is awarded to the winner of the Intermediate Stroke Play Championship. Peg Cross was another who served as Lady Captain and President. • The Coates Trophy is awarded to the winner of the Junior Stroke Play Championship. • The Robyn Cup is awarded to the winner of the C Grade Stroke Play Championship for those handicapped from 41 to 54.

M C Marlow donated this cup.

Trophies associated with Saturday Women’s play include: • The Bluett Trophy donated by Betty Bluett for handicap Match play. • The Goldfinch Plate donated by Mabeth Goldfinch and contested over two Medal rounds on Nett scores.

• The Sheila Morgan Sportswoman Trophy contested via Fourball Match Play.

Trophies associated with Nine Hole Women’s Golf include: • The Women’s Golf Tauranga Cup presented by the Women’s Committee and awarded to the Match Play Champion. • The Sandlant Trophy donated by Phyll Sandlant and awarded to the Stroke Play Champion. Phyll could be regarded as the pioneer sponsor of Women’s Nine Hole Golf. • The Sandlant Dish awarded to the runner up in the Stroke Play.
