1954–1959

Una Wickham and the wider golfing world

A Tauranga player whose achievements connected the club to provincial and national golf.

Una Wickham joined Tauranga Golf Club in 1954 and represented Bay of Plenty in the Russell Grace interprovincial tournament that year. The team won, with further wins recorded in 1955 and 1959.

The book describes Una’s earlier international experience and her interest in more demanding playing conditions for leading women. It also records resistance to her choice of slacks in cold weather, illustrating the conventions women competitors faced.

At club level she began a run of seven consecutive senior championship wins in 1954. The appendix lists her as champion in every year from 1954 through 1959, the end of this chapter’s coverage.

In 1959 she won the New Zealand amateur championship, an achievement identified both in her profile and beside her portrait. Her later record extends well beyond the 1950s and will be developed in a future chapter. The portrait itself is undated; the caption lists titles rather than fixing when the photograph was taken.
