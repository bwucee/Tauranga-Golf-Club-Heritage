THE 2010 CARRUS TAURANGA OPEN CHAMPIONSHIP

Wellington Professional Andrew Green set the 2010 renewal of the Carrus alight with a sizzling 62 in glorious conditions on the opening day. Andrew may well have believed this would give him a substantial lead going into the second round but that was not to be. Southland Amateur Cody Harper (63), and Wellington Professional Brenden Stuart (64) had other ideas.

The Women’s field featured World long-drive champion Phillis Meti who thrilled the gallery with some prodigious driving. Sadly her short game was not of the standard to allow her to take advantage of the opportunities these drives provided.

There are many stories of the par threes at Tauranga. It is notable that David Good, during his record round of 61 in 1976, scored four twos and one three. Club member Eddie Schwartfeger bettered this total of 11 for the par threes in rather dramatic fashion. Playing a Club competition round in June 1982 Eddie scored two pars, a birdie two, and an ace at each of the 9th and 14th holes. Some felt the feat of two aces in the one round may have been a first, and perhaps it was for New Zealand, but a little research discovered that the feat had been achieved over 300 times in America (where else?) the previous year alone.

While no written record has been discovered old time members claim that Jack Bateman, four times Senior Champion and our first BOP Champion of Champions, also bettered David Good by scoring all five par threes in two during a handicap singles match in the early fifties. Jack’s opponent that day would have felt a little shell-shocked.
