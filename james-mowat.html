1936

Professional help, a new chapter

James Mowat and experienced greenkeeper W. H. Coles arrived as the club sought better golf and better playing conditions.

The history reproduces a Bay of Plenty Times report dated 30 March 1936 announcing two appointments: W. H. Coles as greenkeeper and James Mowat as the club’s professional.

Coles had worked at Napier Golf Club. Mowat came from Poverty Bay with experience in Scotland and the United States. The report presented their skills as an opportunity to improve both the course and the standard of play.

Tuition mattered for beginners and established golfers alike. The report emphasised the value of learning sound habits early and having someone able to identify faults in a more experienced player’s swing.

James was Dave Mowat’s father. The book’s introduction acknowledges that family connection, and its later account recalls James supporting junior golfers’ right to enter club championships during the 1950s. His contribution ran beyond the professional appointment itself.
