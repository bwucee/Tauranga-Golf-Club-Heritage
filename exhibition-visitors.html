1960s onwards

A match worth watching

Exhibition matches let members watch leading golfers in a different setting from a full tournament.

The book records Kel Nagle playing Jim Paterson in 1960, the match finishing all square. A year later Bob Charles played the local professional and won two and one.

Another account recalls Peter Thomson playing Fred Ward, including a difficult lie under the boundary hedge. The interest lies as much in the incident and the response as in the outcome.

The chapter shows how the club tried to bring high-quality golf to its members, even when the economics of a single match were modest.

The full exhibition chapter carries the remaining visitors, recollections and details, with the original page references preserved.
